Explore the resist-dyeing process, patterns, and weaving techniques of Ikat textiles.

Historical Overview

Pochampally Ikat, originating from Bhoodan Pochampally in Telangana, is renowned for its geometric patterns and vibrant colours. Also known as Pagdu Bandhu or Chitki, the technique involves tying and dyeing the warp and weft yarns before weaving. This creates the characteristic blurred or feathered edges on the motifs. The craft is deeply intertwined with the history and identity of the region, earning a Geographical Indication (GI) tag in 2005.
