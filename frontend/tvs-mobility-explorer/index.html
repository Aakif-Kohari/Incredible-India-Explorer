THE ORIGIN

From dependable transport to a mobility ecosystem.

The TVS story starts in 1911, when T. V. Sundram Iyengar founded TV Sundram Iyengar & Sons. The organisation later expanded into automotive components and, by the late 1970s, into personal mobility.

The TVS 50 became an important early chapter in affordable Indian two-wheeler transport. Manufacturing, product engineering and racing subsequently became connected parts of the same story.

TVS RACING

The track is the laboratory.

TVS Racing has been active since 1982. The programme grew from a 50 cc moped experiment into rally, road racing, supercross, One Make Championship programmes and international endurance competition.

Its core idea is simple: push machines harder on the track, learn from the data and experience, then bring useful engineering back to road-going motorcycles and scooters.

01 Compete Race against the toughest conditions.
02 Learn Turn race feedback into engineering knowledge.
03 Translate Carry useful performance ideas to road products.
