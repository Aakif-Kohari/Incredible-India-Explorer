Origin & The Visionary Founder

The story of Taj Hotels is inextricably linked with the industrial vision of Jamsetji Nusserwanji Tata, the pioneer of modern Indian industry. Legend dictates that the inception of the brand was catalyzed by an incident of racial discrimination at Mumbai’s erstwhile Watson's Hotel, where Jamsetji was denied entry as an Indian.

Driven by a deep sense of national pride and an ambition to give Bombay a hotel that would rival the finest establishments of Europe, he commissioned the majestic Taj Mahal Palace. Opened on December 16, 1903, it became India's first luxury hotel to feature electricity, American fans, German elevators, and Turkish baths.

Iconic Properties & Palaces

Explore legendary landmarks that define luxury across India's most historic destinations.

Udaipur, Rajasthan

Taj Lake Palace

Floating majestically on Lake Pichola, this 18th-century marble white palace offers breathtaking 360-degree views of the Aravalli hills.

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Rambagh Palace

The former residence of the Maharaja of Jaipur, featuring hand-carved marble lattices, sandstone balustrades, and lush Mughal gardens.

Mumbai, Maharashtra

The Taj Mahal Palace

The crown jewel of Indian hospitality, overlooking the Gateway of India with a blend of Florentine, Moorish, and Oriental architecture.

The Ethos of Indian Hospitality

At the core of the Taj brand philosophy is the timeless Indian tradition of Atithi Devo Bhava ("The Guest is God"). Unlike standardized Western hotel chains, Taj hotels integrate local cultural motifs, classical music, authentic regional gastronomy, and deeply intuitive personalized service.

From palace butlers trained in royal etiquette to sustainable conservation initiatives across heritage wildlife lodges and city sanctuaries, Taj continues to champion the preservation of India's royal and artistic heritage.

Milestones in Evolution

1903

Opening of the iconic Taj Mahal Palace in Mumbai, setting a new benchmark for luxury in Asia.

1970s

Expansion into international markets and management of historic royal palaces like Lake Palace in Udaipur.

2000s

Introduction of sub-brands including Vivanta and Ginger, expanding IHCL's footprint across diverse market segments.

Present Day

Operating a vast global portfolio of world-class hotels, resorts, and palaces under the parent company IHCL.
