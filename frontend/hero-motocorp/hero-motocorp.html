01 · Brand origin

A partnership that changed Indian mobility

Hero Honda Motors Ltd. was incorporated in January 1984. The partnership brought Hero's Indian business experience together with Honda technology and helped establish a high-volume, efficiency-focused motorcycle category.

The CD100, produced in 1985, became the opening chapter of the product story. The company later evolved its identity into Hero MotoCorp after the separation from Honda, with a new corporate identity unveiled in London in 2011.

★
Why the CD100 mattered

Hero's own annual-report timeline describes it as the first motorcycle and highlights its reliable four-stroke engine and value-focused efficiency.
