Anis Kidwai

Writer, Social Worker & Political Figure (1906–1982)

Anis Begum Kidwai was a prominent writer, dedicated social worker, and Member of Parliament who documented the human cost of Partition. Following the tragic murder of her husband by communal mobs in October 1947, she chose the path of reconciliation over bitterness. Meeting Mahatma Gandhi in Delhi, she devoted herself to refugee camps, the recovery of abducted women, and the restoration of communal harmony, recording her experiences in her classic Urdu memoir *Azadi Ki Chhaon Mein*.
