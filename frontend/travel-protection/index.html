05 · EMERGENCY PREPARATION

If something goes wrong

Keep calm, prioritize your safety, and document what happened.

🧳

Lost baggage

Report missing baggage to the airline or transport operator immediately. Keep your baggage claim reference and receipts.

📱

Lost documents

Contact the relevant authority or embassy/consulate and use your backup digital copies where appropriate.

❌

Trip cancellation

Review your booking terms and any applicable protection policy. Keep written cancellation confirmations and receipts.

🚨

Emergency

Move to a safe location and contact local emergency services or trusted contacts when necessary.
