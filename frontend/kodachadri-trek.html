Kodachadri Trek — Karnataka

Experience the enchanting Western Ghats, lush Shola forests, and breathtaking panoramic views.

Trek Profile & Details

  • Location: Shimoga District, Western Ghats, Karnataka
  • Difficulty: Moderate to Difficult
  • Distance & Duration: 14 km (approx. 1-2 Days)
  • Best Season: September to January (Post-monsoon & Winter)
  • Starting Point: Nittur / Kattinahole Village

Natural Features & Biodiversity

Forest & Mountain Landscape

Characterized by dense Shola forests, rolling emerald hills, and steep rugged mountain trails in the Western Ghats.

Flora & Fauna

Home to the rich biodiversity of the Western Ghats, featuring endemic medicinal plants, Malabar Pied Hornbills, and langurs.

Major Viewpoints

Includes the majestic Hindlumane Falls, the historic Sarvajna Peetha at the peak, and stunning sunset viewpoints overlooking the Arabian Sea.

Nearby Attractions

  • Kollur Mookambika Temple
  • Hindlumane Falls
  • Nagara Fort
  • Arasinagundi Falls
