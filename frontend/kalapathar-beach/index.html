Sunrise Coast

Kalapathar Beach

Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman & Nicobar Islands

A rugged, narrow coastline where dense tropical forests collide directly with the marine high-tide line, featuring striking black volcanic rock formations against turquoise waters.

🌐 Destination Profile & Visitor Information

  • Location: Southeastern edge of Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman & Nicobar Archipelago.
  • Landscape: A rugged, narrow coastline alternating between stretches of soft white sand and fields of black volcanic rock formations.
  • Transit Options: Located roughly 6.5 kilometres from the Havelock Island Jetty. The beach can be reached via a 15-minute drive along an oceanside road via rented scooters, auto-rickshaws, or local private cabs.
  • Best Timing: Visitors should aim to arrive between 5:00 AM and 6:30 AM. Due to its eastern exposure, it offers one of the most spectacular sunrise views in the archipelago.

🏄 Major Natural Features & Activities

  • Sunrise Photography: The combination of morning golden hours, black rock formations, and turquoise water makes it a top destination for landscape photographers.
  • Beachcombing & Solitude: Because there are no commercial shops or busy hotels directly on the beach, Kalapathar remains highly peaceful, making it perfect for long walks and reading.
  • Forest Canopy Shading: Towering tropical trees run parallel to the waterline, providing cool, natural shade shelters during midday heat.
  • Swimming Warnings: Caution is advised when entering the water. The underwater bed here contains sharp volcanic rocks and hidden coral blocks. Swimming is generally discouraged during low tides.

🗺️ Nearby Places Worth Visiting

  • Radhanagar Beach (Beach No. 7): Positioned on the western coast of the island, this is the perfect destination to head toward after spending a quiet morning at Kalapathar Beach.
  • Havelock Island Market: A small, lively community hub located midway between the jetty and the beach, ideal for experiencing local culture, fresh coconut water, and authentic seafood.
  • Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep): Accessible via a short cruise from the main Havelock jetty, this nearby island offers a similarly tranquil atmosphere with natural limestone rock bridges.
