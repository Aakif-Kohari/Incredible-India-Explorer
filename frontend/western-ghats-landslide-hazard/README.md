# Western Ghats — Landslide Hazard Profile & Geological Analysis

An interactive digital profile documenting the geological, meteorological, and anthropogenic dimensions of **landslide hazards across the 1,600 km Western Ghats escarpment**.

## Features
- **Key Orographic & Hazard Metrics**: 1,600 km length, Anamudi 2,695 m peak, 4,728 landslides in Kerala (2018), 570 mm / 48 h Wayanad trigger.
- **Interactive Slope Cross-Section Simulator**: Toggle between dry season stability and monsoon saturation with animated perched water tables, pore pressure arrows, and slipping failure blocks.
- **Orographic Monsoon Rainfall Chart**: Monthly comparative bar chart illustrating windward vs leeward precipitation extremes.
- **Interactive Ridge Hotspots Map**: 7 chronic hotspot profiles (*Raigad, Konkan/Satara, Kodagu, Wayanad, Idukki, Malappuram, Nilgiris*).
- **Infrastructure & Environmental Impact Modules**: Road severances, Konkan Railway maintenance, plantation labour colony exposures, and Shola forest fragmentation.
- **Early-Warning Systems Timeline**: GSI nodal mandate (2004), Nilgiris/BGS prototype, NLFC/Bhusanket launch (2024), SLIP-K, and 4.3 lakh sq. km national susceptibility mapping.
- **Interactive Knowledge Check Quiz**: 4-question interactive quiz testing comprehension of failure mechanics, early warning systems, and ecological drivers.
- **Theme Switcher & Semantic SEO**: Dark/Light mode toggle with `localStorage` memory and Schema.org `Article` JSON-LD structured data.

## File Structure
- `index.html`: Semantic HTML5 markup, SVG diagrams, structured data.
- `style.css`: Earthy color palette, responsive typography, and card grids.
- `script.js`: Interactive engine for cross-section toggle, rainfall chart, map pins, and quiz widget.
- `README.md`: Module documentation and technical overview.
