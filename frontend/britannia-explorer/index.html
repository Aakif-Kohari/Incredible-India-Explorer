Britannia (India's Food Brand Journey)

Discover the journey of Britannia — from a small biscuit company in colonial Kolkata in 1892 to one of India's most recognized food brands, spanning biscuits, bakery, dairy, and decades of iconic advertising that shaped Indian food culture.

Historical Origin

Britannia began in 1892 as a small biscuit company in Kolkata (then Calcutta), British India, started with a modest investment. It grew steadily through the early 20th century, coming under ties with the British biscuit maker Peek Freans before supplying biscuits to British forces during the World Wars — a period that significantly scaled up its manufacturing capacity. After India's independence, the Wadia Group's involvement deepened, and Britannia grew into one of India's largest and most trusted packaged food companies.
