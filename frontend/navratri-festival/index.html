Navratri: Nine Nights

Celebrate the divine feminine. Explore the nine forms of Goddess Durga, vibrant Garba nights, and diverse regional traditions across India.

The Navadurga: Nine Forms of the Goddess

Each of the nine nights is dedicated to a specific manifestation of Goddess Durga, representing different virtues, powers, and cosmic principles. Devotees wear specific colors each day and offer specific flowers and foods.

Celebrating Across India

Navratri transforms into distinct cultural phenomena across India's diverse regions, from the ecstatic Garba of Gujarat to the artistic Durga Puja of Bengal.

Garba & Dandiya Raas

The iconic folk dances of Navratri originated in Gujarat but are now celebrated worldwide. Both dances are performed in circles around a centrally lit lamp or image of the Goddess, symbolizing the cyclical nature of time.
