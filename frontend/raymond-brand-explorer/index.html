Raymond India's Textile & Fashion Heritage

Born as a woollen textile mill on the banks of the Ulhas River, Raymond grew over a century into India's most iconic men's fashion and lifestyle brand — weaving together manufacturing heritage, suiting excellence, and the unforgettable identity of The Complete Man.

Raymond — from the Raymond Woollen Mills established in 1925 at Thane, Maharashtra, now India's largest integrated manufacturer of worsted suiting fabric.

Brand Origin & History

1925: A Woollen Mill on the Ulhas River

In 1925, the Raymond Woollen Mills were established at Thane, then a small industrial township near Bombay, on the banks of the Ulhas River. The mill was originally floated by Wadia interests and produced coarse woollen blankets and cloth for the local market.

The pivotal transformation came in 1944, when industrialist Lala Kailashpat Singhania of the prominent J.K. Group acquired the mills. Under his stewardship — and later under his son Vijaypat Singhania — the company undertook a dramatic shift: investing in state-of-the-art machinery imported from Europe and training workers to produce high-quality worsted suiting fabric, which would become Raymond's defining product.

Founding & Leadership

Key Figures in Raymond's History

  • Wadia Group (1925): Originally established Raymond Woollen Mills at Thane to serve local textile demand.
  • Lala Kailashpat Singhania (1944): Acquired the mills under the J.K. Group; initiated modernisation and shift to fine worsted suiting.
  • Vijaypat Singhania (1958–2006): Transformed Raymond from a regional textile mill into a nationally trusted lifestyle brand; launched The Complete Man campaign and diversified into fashion retail.
  • Gautam Singhania (2006–present): Current Chairman and MD; led retail expansion, brand demerger strategy, and omni-channel modernisation.

Wool & Fibre
🐑

Merino Wool & Fine Fibre Sourcing

Raymond sources Merino wool — among the finest natural fibres — primarily from Australia and New Zealand, as well as blended fibres including polyester-wool and linen. This allows production of fabrics ranging from everyday suiting to ultra-luxury Super 250s worsted cloth.

Suiting
🧵

Worsted Suiting Excellence

Raymond's Thane mill complex is India's largest integrated manufacturer of worsted suiting fabric. The company produces over 31 million metres of suiting fabric annually and exports to more than 60 countries, supplying some of the world's finest clothing labels.

Manufacturing
🏭

Vertical Integration & Scale

The Thane manufacturing complex spans over 600 acres and covers every step of textile production — from raw fibre processing to yarn spinning, weaving, dyeing, and finishing. This vertical integration ensures consistent quality across Raymond's entire fabric portfolio.

Advertising Evolution

The Complete Man: India's Most Iconic Fashion Campaign

Launched in 1983 by advertising agency Mudra Communications, The Complete Man campaign positioned Raymond beyond fabric — into aspiration, masculinity, and emotional depth. Created under the vision of Vijaypat Singhania, the campaign depicted a sensitive, successful, well-dressed Indian man — revolutionary for its era.

The tagline "The Complete Man" captured an idea larger than clothing: a man complete in his professional success, his family devotion, and his personal dignity. Over four decades, the campaign evolved from romanticised print ads to emotional television commercials and digital storytelling, remaining one of India's longest-running and most recognised fashion brand campaigns.

Brand Identity

From Mill Brand to Lifestyle Icon

Raymond's brand identity underwent a remarkable transformation across a century. In its early decades, Raymond was purely a textile manufacturer's brand — a mark of origin guaranteeing fabric quality. By the 1970s and 1980s, growing retail presence made Raymond a consumer-facing premium fabric brand known through its showrooms and its fabric's prestige.

The launch of The Complete Man campaign in 1983 pivoted the identity decisively: Raymond became a lifestyle aspiration, not just a textile product. The subsequent decades of brand extensions — Park Avenue for premium menswear, ColorPlus for colour-led casual fashion, Parx for the younger audience, and Ethnix for Indian ethnic wear — built a diversified fashion portfolio all anchored to Raymond's core promise of quality, trust, and premium positioning.

Today Raymond sits at the intersection of heritage and modernity: a century-old manufacturing giant that is simultaneously a contemporary lifestyle and fashion brand with a direct retail network spanning over 1,500 outlets across India and international markets.

Brand Positioning Arc

How the Identity Evolved

  • 1925–1960s: Industrial manufacturer of woollen textiles — brand identity centred on fabric quality and mill origin.
  • 1960s–1982: Premium fabric retailer — Raymond showrooms establish the brand as the preferred choice for tailored suiting across India.
  • 1983–2000: Lifestyle aspiration — "The Complete Man" transforms Raymond into an emotionally resonant, aspirational brand for urban Indian men.
  • 2000–2015: Multi-brand fashion house — Park Avenue, ColorPlus, Parx and KamaSutra extend the brand into diverse fashion segments.
  • 2015–present: Integrated lifestyle brand — Ethnix, omni-channel retail, digital presence, and a real estate demerger sharpen Raymond's focus on fashion and lifestyle.
