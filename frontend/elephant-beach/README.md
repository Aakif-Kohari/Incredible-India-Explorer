# Elephant Beach Explorer — Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep)

## Overview
An interactive destination profile for **Elephant Beach** on Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman & Nicobar Islands — India's premier shallow-water coral reef, snorkeling, sea walking, and tropical rainforest approach destination.

## Key Features & Highlights
- **Shallow Coral Reef & Watersports:** Snorkeling, sea walking, scuba diving, and glass-bottom boat tours.
- **Location & Dual Approach Routes:** Speedboat transit from Havelock Jetty vs. 1.5 km tropical rainforest nature trek.
- **Curated Multi-Day Island Itineraries:** 1-Day Havelock Highlights, 2-Day Adventure & Reef, and 3-Day Swaraj & Shaheed Dweep Circuit.
- **Beaches of India Navigation Matrix:** Regional interactive guide across Andaman, Goa, and Kerala.
- **Visual Photo Gallery & Lightbox:** Interactive gallery with image credits, keyboard navigation (`←`/`→`/`Esc`), and enlarged views.
- **Dual-Theme Support:** Dark Mode and Light Mode with Reef Teal palette.
- **Full SEO & Schema.org Structured Data:** Semantic HTML5 and JSON-LD `@type: "Beach"` markup.
