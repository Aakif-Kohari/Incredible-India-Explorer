Elephant Beach

Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman & Nicobar Islands

The most visited water-sports hub on Havelock Island, Elephant Beach is famous for a shallow coral reef that begins just a few meters from the shore, dense tropical forest fringing the sand, and a full lineup of activities from snorkeling to sea walking.

🌐 Location & Accessibility

  • Location: Northwestern coast of Havelock Island (Swaraj Dweep), Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
  • By Boat: Government-approved speed boats run from Havelock Jetty, taking roughly 20–30 minutes. The ride often passes flying fish and, occasionally, dolphins.
  • By Trek: A scenic ~1.5 km forest trail starting near the Radhanagar Beach road also leads to the beach, best attempted outside monsoon season.
  • Getting there first: Havelock Island itself is reached by ferry from Port Blair, so plan the ferry leg before booking the Elephant Beach boat.
  • Beach Hours: The beach typically closes by early afternoon (around 2:30–2:45 PM), so an early start is recommended to make the most of the visit.

🏖️ Beach Overview

Elephant Beach pairs a wide stretch of white sand with unusually shallow, calm water — the coral reef starts at less than a meter deep in places, which is rare almost anywhere else in the world. That makes it one of the most accessible reef-viewing spots in India, welcoming everyone from first-time snorkelers to experienced divers. The 2004 tsunami reshaped parts of the coastline, but the beach has since remained one of the most-visited stops on any Havelock Island itinerary.

🤿 Marine Activities

  • Snorkeling: The signature activity here — shallow reefs just off the shore make it easy and safe for beginners and non-swimmers, with gear and a short briefing available from local vendors.
  • Scuba Diving: Gentle currents and shallow reefs make Elephant Beach a popular first-dive location for newcomers to diving.
  • Sea Walking: A rare activity in India, letting visitors walk along the seabed in a diving helmet without needing to know how to swim.
  • Jet Skiing & Parasailing: Available at an additional cost for those chasing more of an adrenaline rush.
  • Glass-Bottom Boat Rides & Kayaking: A calmer way to view the coral and marine life for visitors who'd rather stay above the water.

🌴 Natural Attractions

  • Coral Reef Ecosystem: Vibrant, colorful reefs sit just meters from the shoreline, home to a wide variety of reef fish.
  • Tropical Forest Canopy: Dense greenery lines the beach and shades the walking trail, giving the whole approach a jungle-meets-ocean feel.
  • Wildlife En Route: The boat crossing regularly offers glimpses of flying fish, and dolphin sightings are not uncommon.
  • The Beach's Namesake: Elephant Beach gets its name from an old elephant logging/training camp that once operated nearby; no elephants remain on the beach today.

🗺️ Nearby Destinations

  • Radhanagar Beach (Beach No. 7): Connected by the same forest trail, and famous for being one of Asia's most beautiful beaches and its sunset views.
  • Kalapathar Beach: A dramatic coastline of black rocks against aqua-green water, great for early photography.
  • Govind Nagar Beach: A quieter stretch of coastline near the Havelock Jetty area.
  • Havelock Jetty Market: The starting point for most boats to Elephant Beach, with food stalls and local shops nearby.

Seafood Lunch at Havelock Village Market

03:30 PM – 06:30 PM

Radhanagar Beach (Beach No. 7) Sunset

Relax under towering mahua trees on Asia's acclaimed beach and watch the radiant tropical sunset over the Bay of Bengal.

Day 1: Watersports & Radhanagar Sunset

Elephant Beach Snorkeling + Radhanagar Leisure

Complete morning sea walking and glass-bottom boat tour at Elephant Beach, followed by an afternoon swim and sunset at Radhanagar.

Day 2: Kalapathar Sunrise & Jungle Trek

Kalapathar Golden Hour + Elephant Beach Rainforest Trek

Catch early 5:00 AM sunrise over black rocks at Kalapathar Beach, followed by the 1.5 km guided tropical rainforest nature trek to Elephant Beach.

Day 1 – 2: Complete Swaraj Dweep (Havelock)

Elephant Beach, Kalapathar, Radhanagar & Scuba Diving

Full-day water sports, introductory shore dive, sunset adda, and kayak through the mangrove creeks.

Day 3: Shaheed Dweep (Neil Island) Day Trip

Bharatpur Reef & Laxmanpur Natural Rock Bridge

Take morning ferry to Neil Island for coral viewing at Bharatpur and the iconic Howrah Bridge natural coral rock formation at Laxmanpur.
