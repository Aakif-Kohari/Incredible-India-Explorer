01 / Origin

A cooling story rooted in engineering

Voltas was established in 1954 through a collaboration involving Tata Sons and Volkart Brothers. Its history spans engineering, heating, ventilation, air-conditioning, refrigeration and project work, before cooling products became one of its most visible consumer-facing identities.

A

Industrial beginnings

The company grew in an era when India was building industrial, commercial and public infrastructure. Engineering know-how became a foundation for later cooling and building-services work.

B

Cooling expertise

Air-conditioning and refrigeration became important parts of the company's portfolio, linking thermal engineering with practical needs in homes, businesses, institutions and industry.

C

Consumer recognition

Voltas developed a strong consumer association with air conditioners, coolers and other climate-control products while retaining its engineering and projects heritage.
