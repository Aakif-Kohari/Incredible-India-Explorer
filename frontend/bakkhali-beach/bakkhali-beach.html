Bakkhali Beach

A serene crescent-shaped beach on the deltaic islands of the Sundarbans delta, offering tranquil waters and unique coastal landscapes.

Location & Beach Overview

  • Location: South 24 Parganas district, West Bengal, India (located on Bakkhali island, part of the Sundarbans archipelago)
  • Overview: Bakkhali Beach is a peaceful, low-slanted coastline known for its hard-packed sands where visitors can even drive or cycle along the shore, facing the expansive Bay of Bengal.

Natural Environment & Coastal Highlights

Natural Environment

Surrounded by dense casuarina plantations lining the shoreline, mudflats, and rich estuarine biodiversity typical of the lower deltaic regions.

Coastal Highlights

Unlike crowded tourist beaches, Bakkhali offers exceptionally wide stretches of flat sand, gentle tides, and stunning views of sunrises and sunsets over the sea.

Activities & Nearby Attractions

Activities

Enjoy peaceful beach walks, shell collecting, bicycle rides along the hard shore, tasting local Bengali seafood, and bird watching.

Nearby Attractions

Explore the nearby Henry's Island (known for its watchtower and mangrove views), Fraserganj Beach, and the revered Bishalakshmi Temple.
