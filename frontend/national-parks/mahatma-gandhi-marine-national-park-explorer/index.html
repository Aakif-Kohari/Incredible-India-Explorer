Park Overview

History

Established in 1983, the park was named after Mahatma Gandhi to protect the fragile marine ecosystems of the Andaman Islands from human interference.

Coral Reefs

Home to diverse hard and soft coral formations, providing critical habitat for hundreds of marine species and acting as natural coastal barriers.

Mangrove Forests

The park features extensive mangrove ecosystems that serve as nurseries for fish and protect the coastline from erosion and storms.
