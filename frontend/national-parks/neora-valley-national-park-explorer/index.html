Park Overview

Dense Forests

Neora Valley is characterized by thick, untouched temperate and sub-alpine forests, making it one of the most pristine wilderness areas in West Bengal.

Red Panda Habitat

The park is one of the few remaining strongholds for the endangered Red Panda, thriving in the high-altitude bamboo thickets.

Himalayan Wildlife

Home to the Himalayan Black Bear, Clouded Leopard, and a variety of rare avian species adapted to mountainous terrain.
