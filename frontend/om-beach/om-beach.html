Om Beach

A globally renowned coastal gem in Gokarna, uniquely shaped like the auspicious Hindu spiritual symbol 'Om', offering scenic beauty and serene spirituality.

Location & Beach Overview

  • Location: Gokarna, Uttara Kannada District, Karnataka, India
  • Overview: Om Beach is one of Karnataka's most famous coastal destinations, flanked by rocky cliffs, lush green hills, and sweeping golden sand crescents.

Distinctive Beach Shape

The beach derives its name from its naturally curved twin crescent shapes that combine to resemble the sacred Devanagari symbol "Om" (ॐ). Two semi-circular lagoons curve outward, creating a unique and picturesque topography.

Natural Surroundings

Set against a backdrop of rugged Western Ghat hills and rocky outcrops, Om Beach features pristine tidal pools, calm shallow waters, and vibrant surrounding vegetation that transitions smoothly into the deep blue Arabian Sea.

Activities & Nearby Attractions

Activities

Engage in water sports like banana boat rides, jet skiing, parasailing, trekking across coastal cliff trails, and peaceful meditation sessions.

Nearby Attractions

Explore Kudle Beach, Half Moon Beach, Paradise Beach, and the historic Mahabaleshwar Temple in Gokarna town.
