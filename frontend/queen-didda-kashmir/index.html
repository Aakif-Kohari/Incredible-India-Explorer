Queen Didda of Kashmir The Powerful Monarch & Architect of the Lohara Succession

Governing Kashmir for over four decades during the late 10th and early 11th centuries, Queen Didda was one of medieval India's most formidable female monarchs. Bridging the Utpala dynasty and founding the succession of the **Lohara Dynasty**, Didda wielded supreme executive authority as Regent (958–980 CE) and Direct Sovereign Queen (980–1003 CE), subduing rebellious feudal Damaras and safeguarding the valley's sovereignty.
