About the collection

Architecture as a record of every era India has lived through

India's monuments are not a single style but a layered conversation — Buddhist rock-cut sanctuaries, Dravidian temple towers, Rajput hill forts, Indo-Islamic mausoleums, and colonial civic halls, each built on what came before.

The Archaeological Survey of India protects thousands of these sites, but only a fraction are widely known. Heritage Explorer is a starting map: browse by state, by monument type, or by the era that shaped it, then follow any card through to its full story.

"A monument is a sentence the past leaves standing, so that the present has something to read." — on why we protect what we build
