Savitribai Phule

Pioneering Educator, Writer & Reformer (1831–1897)

Savitribai Phule was one of India's most significant pioneers of women's education and social reform in the 19th century. Educated at home by her husband Jyotirao Phule in defiance of strict orthodoxy, she became India's first female teacher in 1848, opening the country's first girls' school at Bhide Wada. She spent her life building schools, establishing refuge homes for widows, fighting caste discrimination, and composing revolutionary Marathi poetry, ultimately sacrificing her life to treat patients during the Pune bubonic plague epidemic.
