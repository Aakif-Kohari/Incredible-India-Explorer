Chandipur: The Vanishing Sea

Walk 5 kilometers into the Bay of Bengal during low tide at Odisha's unique hide-and-seek beach.

Odisha's Hide-and-Seek Beach

Located 16 km from Balasore town in Odisha, Chandipur Beach is one of India's most unique coastal phenomena. During low tide, the sea recedes dramatically up to 5 kilometers, exposing a vast, firm sand bed that visitors can walk, cycle, or even drive vehicles upon.

Geographic Location

Coordinates: 21.47°N, 87.02°E
District: Balasore (Baleswar), Odisha
Coastline: Bay of Bengal
Nearest City: Balasore (16 km), Bhubaneswar (200 km)
Nearest Airport: Bhubaneswar (Biju Patnaik International Airport)

The beach is also famous as a testing site for India's Defense Research and Development Organization (DRDO), where missiles like Prithvi and Akash are test-fired from the Integrated Test Range (ITR) nearby.

The Phenomenon of the Vanishing Sea

What makes Chandipur extraordinary is the dramatic tidal variation. During low tide, the sea water retreats up to 5 km, leaving behind a firm, wet sand bed. During high tide, the water returns, completely submerging the exposed area.

Coastal Ecology

The unique tidal dynamics of Chandipur support a specialized ecosystem. The exposed sand flats during low tide are rich in marine life, while the casuarina groves along the coast provide habitat for coastal birds.
