Plan around the weather

Best season at a glance

Spring

Flowers & fresh trails

Good for many lower and mid-altitude Himalayan routes before the main monsoon.

Summer

High-altitude windows

Higher Himalayan routes can become accessible while many lower trails turn warmer.

Monsoon

Green landscapes

Western Ghats and selected rain-friendly routes become lush; mountain conditions can be hazardous.

Autumn

Clear mountain views

A popular window for Himalayan trekking, with crisp visibility on many routes.

Winter

Snow & cold

Snow routes require appropriate equipment, experience and current local conditions.

Trail planning matters
