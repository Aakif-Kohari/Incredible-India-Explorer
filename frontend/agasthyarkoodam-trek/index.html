Agasthyarkoodam Trek അഗസ്ത്യകൂടം — Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala

Towering 1,868 meters in the Neyyar Wildlife Sanctuary of southern Kerala, Agasthyarkoodam is a prominent peak in the UNESCO Agasthyamala Biosphere Reserve. World-renowned for its pristine tropical rainforests, documented sanctuary of over 2,000 medicinal herbs, and ancient cultural heritage associated with Sage Agastya, this 28km 2-day trek is one of India's most ecologically protected mountain journeys.

UNESCO Agasthyamala Biosphere Reserve

Established as a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve in 2016, Agasthyamala is celebrated for its exceptional concentration of endemic flora and fauna. The mountain slopes host over 2,000 species of medicinal plants, making it a natural treasury of traditional botanical medicine.

Key Features

Highlights of Agasthyarkoodam

High Western Ghats Terrain

Forest Ecosystem & Peak Terrain

The 28km trail transitions through four distinct ecological zones: tropical evergreen rainforests, moist reed brakes, Athirumala high-altitude montane clearing, and the windswept rocky summit cone.

🌲

Tropical Evergreen Rainforests

The lower trail from Bonacaud winds through dense tropical evergreen forest canopy with giant buttressed trees, thick lianas, mountain streams like Karamana river, and moist bamboo groves.

🏕️

Athirumala High Base Camp (1,020m)

Nestled in a high-altitude mountain basin, Athirumala serves as the mandatory overnight halting point. Surrounded by mist-covered peaks, it provides basic Forest Department eco-huts for trekkers.

⛰️

Summit Cone & Rope Climb

The final 4km ascent from Athirumala to the 1,868m summit features steep rocky cliffs, narrow montane ridges, and fixed forest ropes required to negotiate sheer rock sections near the top.

🌊

Water Sources & River Basins

Agasthyarkoodam is the headwater source for major perennial rivers of southern Kerala and Tamil Nadu, including the Karamana, Neyyar, and Thamirabarani river systems.

Ancient Heritage

Sage Agastya & Medicinal Lineage

Agasthyarkoodam holds deep cultural significance in Indian literary and botanical heritage as the traditional abode of Sage Agastya, one of the revered Saptarishis.

📜

Ayurveda & Siddha Medicine Origin

In classical Indian medical traditions, Sage Agastya is celebrated as the founding exponent of Siddha medicine. The mountain's rich concentration of medicinal herbs has attracted botanical scholars and traditional healers for centuries.

🏹

Kani Tribal Heritage

The indigenous Kani tribe has inhabited the forest valleys surrounding Agasthyarkoodam for generations. Their deep ethnobotanical knowledge led to the scientific documentation of *Arogyapacha* (*Trichopus zeylanicus*), sharing benefit-sharing royalties under India's biodiversity framework.

Eco-Tourism Regulations

Forest Department Entry Permits & Safety Rules

Due to its status as a core wildlife sanctuary and fragile biosphere reserve, entry to Agasthyarkoodam is strictly regulated by the Kerala Forest & Wildlife Department.
