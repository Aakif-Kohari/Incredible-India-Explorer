Rajgad Fort Trek राजगड — King of Forts (Maratha Capital)

Standing 1,376 meters high in the Sahyadri mountains, Rajgad was the beating heart and imperial capital of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's empire for 26 years. Explore three sprawling fortified machis, the ultra-steep Bale Killa citadel, and historic stone gateways.

1,376 m Summit Elevation (4,514 ft)
26 Years Capital of Maratha Empire
3.5 - 4 hrs One-Way Ascent Time

Architectural & Defensive Masterpiece

Key Highlights of Rajgad

Discover Padmavati Machi, the 2.5km triple curtain walls of Sanjeevani Machi, Suvela Machi's needle-eye rock hole (Nedhe), and the vertical Bale Killa citadel.

Sahyadri Trail Breakdown

Gunjavane & Pali Route Guide

Trekkers can ascend via Gunjavane village (steep rock scramble into Chor Darwaja) or via Pali village (royal stone steps leading through Pali Darwaja).

🏔️ Legendary Range Trek: Torna to Rajgad Trail

Connect the first fort of Swarajya (Torna) with the capital (Rajgad) along a high 15 km Sahyadri mountain ridge.

  • Route: Torna (Budhla Machi) → Chela Wato → Rajgad (Sanjeevani Machi).
  • Duration: 6 to 8 hours of continuous ridge traversal.
  • Pro Tip: Overnight stay in Padmavati Temple on Rajgad after completing the ridge trek.
26 Years of Sovereign Power

Historical Timeline of Rajgad

From 1647 to 1673 CE, Rajgad served as Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's capital city, witnessing historic battles, strategic treaties, and his heroic return from Agra.

🚌 Transportation Options

  • From Pune: MSRTC buses operate from Swargate to Gunjavane or Margasani (~2 hours).
  • By Car / Bike: Swargate → Nasrapur phata → Margasani → Gunjavane or Pali (approx 60 km).
  • Parking: Vehicle parking available at both Gunjavane and Pali base villages.

🛏️ Overnight Shelter & Meals

  • Padmavati Temple Shelter: Accommodates 50+ trekkers for night stays inside temple halls.
  • Local Village Food: Villagers provide hot Pithla Bhakri, Thecha, and tea on Padmavati Machi.
  • Drinking Water: Fresh potable water available at Padmavati Lake cisterns.
