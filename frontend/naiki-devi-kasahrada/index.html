Queen Naiki Devi & The Battle of Kasahrada Chaulukya Resistance & The Defeat of Ghori at Mount Abu (1178 CE)

In 1178 CE, the Ghurid ruler Shihab ad-Din Muhammad Ghori led an army across the Thar Desert into northern Gujarat. Standing in defense of the realm was **Queen Naiki Devi**, the Kadamba princess turned Queen Regent of the **Chaulukya (Solanki) Dynasty**, governing on behalf of her young minor son **King Mularaja II**. At the mountain defile of Kasahrada (Gadaraghatta pass near Mount Abu), the allied Chaulukya forces inflicted a decisive defeat on Ghori's cavalry, repelling the invader and securing 12th-century Gujarat.

The Chaulukya Victory at Gadaraghatta Pass

The Battle of Kasahrada (fought near Kayadra village at the foot of Mount Abu) stands as one of medieval India's most notable defensive victories. Under the regency of Queen Naiki Devi and the minor King Mularaja II, a united coalition of Chaulukya troops and regional Rajput feudatories routed Muhammad Ghori's desert-weary forces, forcing a complete Ghurid retreat to Multan.
