Global Impact

Indian cinema has transcended borders. From Satyajit Ray winning an Honorary Academy Award and Lagaan's historic Oscar nomination, to RRR winning Best Original Song and the global dominance of Bollywood dance sequences—India's cinematic influence is recognized at major festivals (Cannes, Venice, TIFF) and celebrated by a massive global diaspora.
