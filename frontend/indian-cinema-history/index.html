Global Impact

Indian cinema has influenced audiences worldwide through international film festivals, diaspora audiences, and the growth of streaming platforms. From Satyajit Ray's international recognition to the historic Academy Award win for RRR's "Naatu Naatu", Indian films continue to gain global acclaim.

Did You Know?

India produces between 1,500 to 2,000 films every year, making it the largest film industry in the world by output.
