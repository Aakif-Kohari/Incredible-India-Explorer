👋 Traditional Greeting IPA: /kʰu.lum.kʰa/

Khulumkha!

Koloma: 𑰏𑰲𑰩𑰲𑰦𑰏𑰯 Bengali: খুলুমখা Roman: Khulumkha
"Hello / Warmest Greetings / Welcome"

Performed with folded hands, Khulumkha is the fundamental Tripuri expression of respect, peace, and traditional hospitality.
