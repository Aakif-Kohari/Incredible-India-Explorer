Discover Kheerganga Trek

Kheerganga is one of the most popular trekking destinations in Himachal Pradesh, located at the extreme end of the Parvati Valley. It represents a beautiful blend of deep natural wilderness and intense Hindu spiritual heritage.

The trek starts from Barsheni village and winds upward through thick pine, deodar, and oak forests. Enroute, hikers pass the ancient wooden hamlet of Nakthan, roaring waterfalls, and the sacred temple of Rudranag. The final destination is a green meadow at 2,960m containing the natural sulphur hot springs of Parvati Kund, where trekkers can take a warm, relaxing bath looking out at snow-covered peaks.

Major Attractions & Highlights

💡 Quick Trek Tips

  • Hot Spring Rules: Separate bathing pools are constructed for men and women. Do not use chemical soap or shampoo inside the pool.
  • Accommodation: Permanent commercial tents are banned at the top meadow. Simple wooden homestays and camping are available at lower clearings and Barsheni.
  • Nearby Villages: Visit Tosh, Kalga, and Pulga villages near the base for traditional Himachali food and architecture.
  • Best Season: April to June and September to November. Winters are very cold and receive heavy snow blockades.

Trek Route & Day-wise Highlights

The trail climbs from Barsheni checkpost, passing the Nakthan village flat, crossing the river at Rudranag temple, and taking a steep forest ascent to the top meadow.

History & Cultural Context

According to legend, Lord Kartikeya meditated here for thousands of years. Shiva created a flow of milk (kheer) to feed him, which later became the white sulphur hot springs. Nath saints have managed ashrams here for centuries.
