Usri Falls

Follow the forest stream of Giridih. A hidden three-tiered cascade nestled in the rugged hills of Jharkhand.

The Forest Stream of Giridih

Located in the Giridih district of Jharkhand, Usri Falls is a stunning natural wonder where the Usri River descends through a narrow gorge in three distinct streams. The name "Usri" is derived from the local dialect, meaning "a river that flows through rocks."

Geological Setting

The falls are carved into ancient granite and gneiss rocks of the Chota Nagpur Plateau. The river forces its way through a steep, narrow gorge, creating a dramatic 12-meter drop surrounded by dense, deciduous forests.

Seasonal Transformation

Usri Falls undergoes a dramatic transformation depending on the season, shifting from a roaring monsoon cascade to a serene, rocky stream in summer.
