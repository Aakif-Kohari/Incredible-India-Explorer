# 👩‍💼 Sucheta Kriplani: Pioneer of Women's Leadership

## Overview
A comprehensive historical profile of **Sucheta Kriplani** (1908-1974), freedom fighter, Constituent Assembly member, and India's first woman Chief Minister. This explorer documents her remarkable journey from nationalist family to breaking the glass ceiling in Indian politics.

## Features
- **Early Life:** 6 features covering family background and nationalist upbringing
- **Education:** 6 aspects of educational journey and formative influences
- **Freedom Struggle:** 6 features and 6 contributions to independence movement
- **Quit India Movement:** 6 features and 6 contributions to 1942 movement
- **Constituent Assembly:** 6 features and 6 contributions to constitution drafting
- **Political Career:** 6 features and 6 parliamentary contributions
- **Chief Minister:** 6 features and 6 initiatives as UP's CM (1963-1967)
- **Timeline:** 10 key events from birth to legacy
- **Legacy:** 6 enduring contributions and 6 continuing impacts
- **Gallery:** 12 historical images with lightbox modal

## Key Achievements
- First woman Chief Minister of India (Uttar Pradesh, 1963-1967)
- One of 15 women in Constituent Assembly
- Multiple-term Member of Parliament
- Active participant in Quit India Movement
- International representative at UN
