# Mandarmani Beach Explorer — West Bengal

## Overview
An interactive destination profile for **Mandarmani Beach** in Purba Medinipur, West Bengal — a 13-kilometre shoreline of hard, compact sand widely known as India's longest drivable beach, famed for its thousands of red ghost crabs and seaside resorts.

## Key Features & Highlights
- **Mile-Marker Drivebar Navigation:** Interactive sticky navigation tracking beach distance from 0 KM to 13 KM.
- **Beach Characteristics & Drivable Sand:** Hard-packed terrain analysis, low gentle surf, and weather season widget.
- **Tide Dynamics & Driveability Safety Widget:** Low tide vs. high tide guidance for beach exploration.
- **Curated Multi-Day Itineraries:** 1-Day Quick Seaside Escape, 2-Day Coastal Trio (Mandarmani + Tajpur + Shankarpur), and 3-Day East Medinipur Circuit.
- **Illustrated Vector SVG Gallery & Modal Lightbox:** Hand-crafted vector artwork capturing dawn sunrises, red crabs, dune driving, and fishing boats with interactive lightbox controls.
- **Local Highlights & Seafood Guide:** Fresh pomfret, chili crab, and prawn delicacies by the waves.
- **Full SEO & Schema.org Structured Data:** Semantic HTML5 with JSON-LD `@type: "Beach"` markup.
