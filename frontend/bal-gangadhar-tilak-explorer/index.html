Bal Gangadhar Tilak

The Father of Indian Unrest

Awakening a nation with the immortal call for Swaraj.

"Swaraj is my birthright, and I shall have it!"

Declared in 1916 at Belgaum, this slogan became the rallying cry for the Indian independence movement, shifting the demand from mere administrative reforms to complete self-rule.

Biography

Bal Gangadhar Tilak (1856–1920) was one of the first and strongest advocates of Swaraj (self-rule) and a strong radical in Indian consciousness.

Born in Ratnagiri, Maharashtra, Tilak began his career as an educator, co-founding the Deccan Education Society to improve the quality of education for Indian youth and instill nationalistic ideals.

He was part of the famous extremist triumvirate 'Lal Bal Pal' (along with Lala Lajpat Rai and Bipin Chandra Pal). Tilak transformed the anti-imperialist struggle from a movement of the upper classes into a mass movement.
