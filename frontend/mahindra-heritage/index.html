🚙 Mahindra: Forging India's Rugged Mobility

Explore the robust evolution of Mahindra & Mahindra, from assembling Willys Jeeps to becoming a global force in utility vehicles, farm equipment, and sustainable mobility.

The Origins: From Steel to Jeeps

Founded in 1945 as Mahindra & Muhammad by brothers K.C. Mahindra and J.C. Mahindra along with Malik Ghulam Muhammad. Initially a steel trading company, they saw a massive opportunity in bringing rugged utility vehicles to India. After India's partition, the company was renamed Mahindra & Mahindra in 1948 and began assembling the iconic Willys Jeep, laying the foundation for their legendary rugged DNA.

🛣️

Utility Vehicles (UVs)

Mahindra is synonymous with Utility Vehicles in India. From the classic Commander to the rugged Bolero, the urban-friendly Scorpio, and the modern XUV series, Mahindra has dominated the UV segment by offering vehicles built for Indian roads and families.

🚜

Farm Equipment

Entering the tractor market in 1963, Mahindra recognized the critical need for mechanizing Indian agriculture. Today, it stands as the largest tractor manufacturer in the world by volume, driving rural prosperity across the globe.

Brand Expansion & Sustainable Future

Beyond automotive and farm equipment, the Mahindra Group has expanded into IT (Tech Mahindra), aerospace, defense, and real estate. Notably, Mahindra pioneered electric mobility in India through Mahindra Reva (now Mahindra Electric) and continues to invest heavily in the Born Electric (BE) range of future vehicles.

Interactive Vehicle Evolution Timeline

Scroll down to explore major milestones and iconic vehicles in Mahindra's rich history.

1947

Willys Jeep Assembly

Mahindra brought the legendary Willys Jeep to Indian shores, initially importing them as Completely Knocked Down (CKD) kits and assembling them, establishing its reputation for robust, go-anywhere vehicles.

2000

Mahindra Bolero

The launch of the Mahindra Bolero. With its rugged build and reliable engine, it quickly became India's most popular utility vehicle, dominating rural and semi-urban markets.

2002

Mahindra Scorpio

A watershed moment for the company. The Scorpio was Mahindra's first global SUV, developed entirely in-house. It redefined the brand's image, proving they could build modern, aspirational vehicles.

2010

Mahindra Thar

The introduction of the Thar, a modern successor to their classic Jeeps. It immediately gained a cult following among off-road enthusiasts and lifestyle buyers.

2011

Mahindra XUV500

Mahindra's first monocoque SUV. Packed with features and an aggressive cheetah-inspired design, it became a massive success and elevated the brand into the premium SUV segment.

2021

Mahindra XUV700 & The New Logo

The launch of the technologically advanced XUV700, featuring ADAS and luxury features. This vehicle also debuted Mahindra's new 'Twin Peaks' logo, representing a modern, global identity.
