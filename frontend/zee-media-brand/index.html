📺 Zee: India's Pioneering Media Brand

Trace the incredible journey of Zee Entertainment Enterprises, from launching India's first Hindi satellite channel to becoming a global digital powerhouse.

October 1992

The Origin: Launch of Zee TV

Founded by Subhash Chandra, Zee Telefilms launched Zee TV, becoming India's first Hindi-language cable and satellite television channel. This groundbreaking move revolutionized the Indian broadcasting industry, breaking the monopoly of the state-owned Doordarshan.

April 1995

Zee Cinema: India's First Movie Channel

Zee expanded its portfolio by launching Zee Cinema, India's first 24-hour Hindi movie channel. It capitalized on the massive popularity of Bollywood, bringing cinema directly into the living rooms of millions of Indians.

1999

The Birth of Zee News

Recognizing the need for private news broadcasting, Zee News was launched as a 24-hour news channel. It set new standards for journalism and live news reporting in India.

November 2006

Corporate Evolution: ZEE Entertainment

Zee Telefilms underwent a strategic restructuring and was renamed Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd (ZEEL). This marked its shift from a pure broadcaster to a comprehensive media and entertainment conglomerate.

2012

Early Digital Steps: DittoTV

Zee launched DittoTV, one of India's earliest Over-The-Top (OTT) platforms. It allowed users to watch live TV and catch-up content on their mobile devices, signaling Zee's early recognition of the digital future.

February 2018

Digital Transformation: Launch of ZEE5

Zee consolidated its digital offerings (DittoTV and OZEE) to launch ZEE5. The platform offered an extensive library of original shows, movies, and live TV across 12 languages, aggressively positioning Zee in the competitive Indian streaming market.

2021 & Beyond

Global Expansion & Major Milestones

Zee's network expanded to over 100 channels in multiple languages, reaching over 1.3 billion viewers globally across 190+ countries. The company continues to pioneer content creation, digital distribution, and global media partnerships.
