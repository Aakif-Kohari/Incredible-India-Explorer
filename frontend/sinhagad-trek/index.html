Sinhagad Fort Trek सिंहगड — The Lion's Fort (Kondhana)

Standing 1,312 meters tall in the Sahyadris, Sinhagad is Pune's most beloved fortress trek. Immortalized by Subedar Tanaji Malusare's heroic night scaling of Tanaji Kada in 1670 CE, it blends steep stone steps, ancient gateways, and iconic Maharashtrian mountain gastronomy.

Architectural & Natural Wonders

Key Highlights of Sinhagad Fort

Discover Tanaji Kada cliff face, Kalyan and Pune Gateways, ancient rock-cut water springs, and sacred memorials set against sweeping views of Khadakwasla backwaters.

Trail Breakdown

Atekarwadi Base to Kalyan Darwaja Route

The popular trekking trail ascends from Atekarwadi base village near Donje, following red soil slopes and breezy ridges before climbing stone steps into Kalyan Darwaja.

The Valor of Maratha Mavals

Historical Timeline of Sinhagad

From 14th-century Koli rulers to the legendary 1670 night battle led by Subedar Tanaji Malusare, Sinhagad has stood as a guardian fortress of Maharashtra.

Food Heritage

Famous Summit Culinary Specialties

No trek to Sinhagad is complete without indulging in the traditional rustic delicacies served fresh by local villagers at the summit.

🧅

Hot Kanda Bhajji

Crispy onion fritters fried fresh in iron kadhais, best paired with hot tea during foggy mornings.

🍲

Pithla Bhakri

Spiced gram flour curry served with wood-fired Jowar or Bajra flatbread and spicy garlic-chilli Thecha.

🏺

Mattha & Earthen Dahi

Fresh thick curds set in clay pots (Matkas) and digestive spiced buttermilk flavored with cumin and coriander.
