Discover Beas Kund Trek

Beas Kund is a revered, high-altitude alpine lake nestled at the head of Solang Valley near Manali in Himachal Pradesh. Spanning across the transition zone between the Dhauladhar and Pir Panjal mountain ranges, this glacier tarn serves as the primary hydrological source of the Beas River, locally known as the Vipasha.

The trail begins near the road terminus of Dhundi and climbs alongside the cascading infant Beas stream. Trekkers walk through ancient silver birch (Bhojpatra) and coniferous forests, emerging onto the high grassy grasslands of Bakarthach, where local Gaddi shepherds camp during the summer. Beyond the tree line, the trail climbs up steep moraines to reach the turquoise glacial lake, ringed by giant climbing peaks.

Major Attractions & Highlights

💡 Quick Trek Tips

  • Permit Requirements: No special forest permit is required for Indian nationals, but checking in at the Dhundi Forest/Police checkpost is mandatory.
  • Camping Spots: Bakarthach (3,270m) is the most popular campsite. Camping directly next to the sacred lake is discouraged to preserve water purity.
  • Nearby Excursions: Jogini Waterfall, Rohtang Pass, Vashisht hot water springs, and paragliding takeoffs at Solang Valley.
  • Best Time: May to June (pre-monsoon snow views) and September to October (clear post-monsoon skies).

Trek Route & Day-wise Highlights

The standard trail starts from the Dhundi checkpost, climbs up to Bakarthach meadows, and ascends the moraine ridge to Beas Kund.
