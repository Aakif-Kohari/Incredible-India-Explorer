Explore Kynrem Falls

Nestled inside Thangkharang Park near Sohra (Cherrapunji), Meghalaya. Kynrem Falls cascades in three spectacular tiers down the East Khasi Hills into the plains of Bangladesh.

Peak Monsoon Surge

During peak monsoon in Cherrapunji (one of the wettest places on Earth), Kynrem Falls transforms into a roaring, thunderous torrent visible miles away, filling the entire gorge with dense mist and rainbows.

Flow Volume: 100% Maximum Best Viewpoint: Thangkharang Park Gate Travel Tip: Carry heavy rain gear

Visitor Information & Guidelines

Everything you need to know before visiting Kynrem Falls.

🕒 Timings & Entry

Open: 6:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Daily)
Entry Fee: ₹30 per person (Thangkharang Park)
Camera Fee: ₹50 for DSLR/Professional gear

🚗 How to Reach

By Air: Shillong Airport (Umroi) is ~65 km away.
By Train: Guwahati Railway Station (~160 km).
By Road: Well-connected from Shillong via NH6 (approx. 60 km, 2 hours).

⚠️ Safety & Guidelines

Stay behind designated safety barriers. Do not attempt to climb the slippery rocks near the plunge pool. Carry leech socks during the monsoon season.
