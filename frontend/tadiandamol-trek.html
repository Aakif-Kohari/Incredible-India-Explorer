Tadiandamol Trek — Karnataka

Conquer the highest peak of Kodagu, wandering through coffee estates and verdant Shola forests.

Trek Profile & Details

  • Location: Kodagu (Coorg) District, Western Ghats, Karnataka
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate
  • Distance & Duration: 12 km (approx. 1 Day)
  • Best Season: September to March
  • Starting Point: Nalaknad Palace / Kakkabe

Natural Features & Biodiversity

Forest & Mountain Landscape

Experience the gentle rolling green hills of the Western Ghats, interspersed with dense Shola forests and coffee plantations.

Flora & Fauna

Rich in biodiversity, the trail offers glimpses of rare birdlife, endemic flora of the Western Ghats, and expansive coffee estates.

Major Viewpoints

The Tadiandamol peak provides breathtaking, panoramic views of the Western Ghats extending into the distant Kerala borders on a clear day.

Nearby Attractions

  • Nalaknad Palace
  • Chelavara Falls
  • Padi Igguthappa Temple
  • Kakkabe Village
