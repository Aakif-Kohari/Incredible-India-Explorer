Rishikonda Beach

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

The jewel of the Visakhapatnam coastline. The only beach in Andhra Pradesh to hold the prestigious international Blue Flag certification, beautifully blending raw natural landscapes with high-energy water sports.

🌐 Destination Profile & Geographical Features

  • Location: Around 11 kilometers north of Visakhapatnam city center, along the scenic Beach Road.
  • Natural Landscape: A pristine, golden-sand crescent bay framed by majestic hills. The beach features a gentle marine shelf and relatively clear, low-sediment waters compared to other urban spots in the region, making it visually striking and structurally distinct.
  • The Blue Flag Standard: Thanks to its eco-certified status, the beach offers clean solar-powered changing facilities, continuous water-quality monitoring, clean pedestrian tracks, and professional first-aid and safety infrastructure.

🏄 Major Recreational Activities & Attractions

  • Water Sports Capital: Rishikonda is the primary hub for adventure sports in Vizag. Visitors can participate in sea kayaking, speedboating, jet-skiing, and windsurfing under the supervision of certified local instructors.
  • Safe Swimming Zones: Unlike Ramakrishna Beach, Rishikonda features a dedicated, patrolled swimming area with gentle surf breaks, making it highly accessible for families and casual waders.
  • Sunrise Walking Paths: The beach promenade offers unobstructed panoramic views of sunrises over the Bay of Bengal, making it a favorite spot for morning fitness enthusiasts and landscape photographers.

🗺️ Nearby Places Worth Visiting

  • Thotlakonda Buddhist Complex: Perched on a hilltop just a few kilometers away, this ancient 2nd-century BC monastic archaeological site offers panoramic coastal views and rich historical insights.
  • Kailasagiri Hill Park: A panoramic hilltop park featuring massive sculptures of Shiva and Parvati, manicured gardens, and a toy train ride overlooking the curving Visakhapatnam shoreline.
  • INS Kursura Submarine Museum: Located an easy 15-minute drive down the coastal highway on RK Beach, providing an immersive look at submarine engineering.
