Rani Abbakka Chowta I The Courageous Queen of Ullal Who Resisted Portuguese Expansion

One of India's earliest anti-colonial figures, Rani Abbakka Chowta I ruled the prosperous spice port of Ullal on the Karnataka coast. Inheriting the crown through the matrilineal Aliyasantana tradition of Tulu Nadu, she defied Portuguese trade monopolies, rejected custom custom toll demands, and commanded a multi-religious naval coalition that repulsed repeated European campaigns for over two decades.

Preserving Spice Independence against Maritime Hegemonies

Rani Abbakka Chowta stands out in Indian history as a ruler who resisted foreign maritime blockades. Rejecting custom tax cartazes and trading directly with Arabia and Calicut, she organized Ullal's local defense through regional alliances and strategic guerilla operations.
