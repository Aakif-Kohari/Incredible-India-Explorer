Bhikaji Cama

The revolutionary who carried India's call for freedom beyond its shores.

01

A Flag Beyond India's Shores

Bhikaji Cama was one of the prominent Indian nationalists who carried the message of India's independence to the international stage.

Living in Europe for many years, she worked with Indian revolutionaries and used international platforms to draw attention to India's struggle against British rule.

In 1907, she became closely associated with a historic flag presentation at the International Socialist Congress in Stuttgart, Germany.

02

Her Story

01 / 04

From Bombay to the World

Bhikaji Cama was born in Bombay in 1861. She later became deeply involved in the Indian nationalist movement and eventually worked from Europe.

02 / 04

Taking the Struggle Abroad

From Europe, Cama collaborated with Indian nationalists and revolutionary thinkers. She used speeches, writing and political networks to highlight the condition of India under British rule.

03 / 04

The Stuttgart Moment

In 1907, at the International Socialist Congress in Stuttgart, Germany, Cama presented a version of an Indian nationalist flag and spoke about India's struggle for freedom.

04 / 04

A Symbol of Resistance

The flag became an important symbol in the history of India's nationalist movement. Cama's international activism helped make India's independence struggle visible beyond the country.
