Kovalam Beach

A crescent-shaped paradise in Kerala, famous for its pristine shores, iconic lighthouse, and world-renowned Ayurvedic wellness retreats.

Coastal Paradise

Located just 16 km from Thiruvananthapuram, Kovalam is one of India's most internationally recognized beach destinations. Its name translates to "coconut grove," reflecting the endless canopy of palm trees that line its golden sands.

Geographical Features

Kovalam's coastline is uniquely divided by rocky headlands into three distinct crescent-shaped bays. The shallow waters and natural coral reefs make it exceptionally safe for swimming and water sports, while the steep palm-covered cliffs offer panoramic views of the Arabian Sea.
