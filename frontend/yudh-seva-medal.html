Yudh Seva Medal (YSM)

Awarded to personnel of the Armed Forces for distinguished service of a high order during war, conflict, or hostilities.

1980 Instituted
GoI Awarded By

History & Legacy

Instituted by the Government of India in 1980, the Yudh Seva Medal (YSM) is one of India's esteemed wartime distinguished service decorations. It was established to recognize and reward distinguished service of a high order rendered in an operational theatre during times of war, conflict, or hostilities.

Eligibility Criteria

Personnel eligible for the Yudh Seva Medal include all ranks of the following forces:

  • Indian Army
  • Indian Navy
  • Indian Air Force
  • Territorial Army
  • Reserve and Auxiliary Forces
  • Other personnel lawfully embodied under the direction of the Government of India during operations

Medal Design

Appearance: Circular in shape, typically made of standard medal alloys, with a distinct military finish.

National Emblem: The obverse bears the State Emblem of India, while the reverse is inscribed with "Yudh Seva Medal" in Hindi and English.

Ribbon Pattern: A distinctive ribbon featuring stripes that distinguish it from peacetime service medals.

Symbolism: Represents steadfastness, operational leadership, and unwavering commitment during wartime.

Wartime Operations

The YSM has historically been awarded for distinguished service during critical operations such as:

  • Major war operations and cross-border conflicts
  • Intensive counter-insurgency operations
  • Joint service military operations
  • Strategic command and operational leadership
  • High-risk military missions in hostile environments

Selection Process

Interesting Facts

  • 🎖️ Acts as a wartime equivalent to the Vishisht Seva Medal (VSM).
  • ⚔️ Awarded for exceptional leadership and service directly related to a conflict.
  • 🪖 Can be awarded to any branch of the Armed Forces, including nursing officers and reserve forces.
  • 📜 Usually announced by the Government of India on designated national occasions like Republic Day or Independence Day.
