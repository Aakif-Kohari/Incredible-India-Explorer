Treasures Unique to the Subcontinent

Endemic species are plants and animals that exist naturally in one specific geographic region and nowhere else in the wild. India's vast ecosystems — from the Western Ghats and Himalayas to the Andaman Islands — harbor extraordinary species found exclusively on Indian soil.
