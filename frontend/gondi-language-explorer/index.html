Seva Johar

✨ "Respectful Salutations"

The traditional greeting exchanged across Koitur (Gond) communities, often chanted together at community gatherings and cultural events.

Johar
[dʒoːɦaːr]

Koitur
[koː.iː.t̪oːr]
🪶 "We are Koitur" — a statement of identity

Often spoken with pride when introducing oneself, asserting the community's own name over the outsider term "Gond".

✍️ Script & Writing Systems

Gondi Script Information

A historically oral language with two remarkable indigenous scripts of its own.

📜

Oral Tradition & Adopted Scripts

Gondi has a rich oral tradition of folk songs, wedding narrations and clan genealogies, but historically little was written down.

Where Gondi is written today, it most commonly borrows the Devanagari script (देवनागरी) in Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh and Maharashtra, and the Telugu script (తెలుగు) in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Devanagari Script Rendering: गोंडी भाषा
Latin Phonetic Notation: Gōṇḍī Bhāṣā
IPA International Phonetic: [ɡoːɳɖiː bʱaːʂaː]
🖋️

Two Indigenous Gondi Scripts

  • Gunjala Gondi Script: A dozen manuscripts dated to around 1750 were rediscovered in 2014 in Gunjala village, Adilabad district. The script was added to the Unicode Standard in 2018 and is now being revived and taught in select schools.
  • Masaram Gondi Script: Devised in 1918 by Munshi Mangal Singh Masaram of Madhya Pradesh, this is a separately created alphabet promoted for writing Gondi.
  • Consonant System: Gondi has developed a full set of aspirated stops (pʰ, tʰ, kʰ, bʱ, dʱ, ɡʱ), a feature that distances it from its Proto-Dravidian ancestor.
  • Retroflex Sounds: Consonants like ɳ, ʈ, ɖ, ɽ are pronounced with the tongue curled back, audible in words like Gōṇḍī itself.

🌳 Linguistic Family Lineage

Dravidian Language Family Context

Gondi belongs to the South-Central Dravidian branch, in the same Telugu-Gondi subgroup as Telugu itself.

Dravidian Language Tree Classification

The Origin of the Name "Gond"

The very name "Gond" traces a path through the region's Dravidian and Indo-Aryan neighbours:

Source Language Term Meaning
Telugu Hill
Sanskrit Mountain inhabitant
Self-designation (Gondi) The People

The Koitur themselves reject "Gond" as an outsider label rooted in "hill people" — preferring their own name, Koitur, meaning simply "the People".

🔥 Koitur Heritage

Gondi Culture, Kingdoms & Traditions

Discover the history, faith and art forms of India's largest Scheduled Tribe.

👑

The Gond Kingdoms

Between the 14th and 18th centuries, powerful Gond kingdoms such as Garha-Mandla, Deogarh and Chanda ruled large parts of central India, with the warrior-queen Rani Durgavati famed for resisting Mughal expansion.

🔥

Koya Punem Faith

The indigenous Koya Punem belief system centres on nature worship, ancestor reverence, and the supreme deity Persa Pen, distinct from mainstream Hindu practice.

🎨

Pardhan Gond Art & Music

The Pardhan community, hereditary bards of the Gonds, preserve oral epics through song. Their tradition also gave rise to the celebrated Pardhan Gond painting movement of intricate dotted and lined artwork.

📜

Gunjala Script Revival

Rediscovered manuscripts from 1750 revealed the long-lost Gunjala Gondi script. Added to Unicode in 2018, it is now taught to a new generation of students in parts of Telangana.
