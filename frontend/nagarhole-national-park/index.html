Park overview

Where forest wildlife meets the Kabini waters

Nagarhole National Park is presented as one of India's premier wildlife reserves. Its story combines tiger reserve forests, elephant movement, the Kabini River, bird habitats, safari interpretation and biodiversity conservation.

Kabini River landscape

The Kabini river and backwater system creates a powerful ecological edge. Water attracts herbivores, birds, reptiles and predators, making the river central to the Nagarhole experience.

Tiger reserve

Nagarhole's protected forest supports predator-prey relationships, tiger conservation and connected wildlife movement.

Elephants and birds

Elephants and birdlife make the landscape especially rich for safari learning and forest-river biodiversity awareness.
