Kedar Ghat

The Southern Sacred Steps of Varanasi & The Himalayan Sanctum on the Ganga

Distinguished by its towering vertical red-and-white striped temple facade and steep sandstone steps, Kedar Ghat is one of the most spiritually venerated riverfronts in Varanasi. Crowning the steps is the ancient Sri Kedareshwar Temple, housing a self-manifested (*Svayambhu*) lingam that replicates the sacred sanctity of Kedarnath in the Himalayas right on the holy banks of the Ganga.

🌊 Geographic Setting & Historical Foundations

📍 Riverfront Location

Located in the southern segment of Varanasi between Harishchandra Ghat and Chauki Ghat, Kedar Ghat serves as the epicentre of the historic Kedar Khanda, one of Kashi's three sacred theological zones.

📜 Ancient Puranic Lineage

Celebrated in the Skanda Purana (Kashi Khanda), Kedar Ghat is anciently chronicled as a primary tirtha where taking a holy dip yields the same spiritual merit as trekking to the Himalayan shrine of Kedarnath.

🧱 Stone Architectural Reconstruction

Substantially renovated with solid stone steps in the late 16th century and expanded by the Maharaja of Vijayanagaram and Bengali-South Indian patrons, the ghat features durable stone retaining bastions and sacred bathing platforms.

🛕 Temple Traditions, Religious Significance & Architecture

Sri Kedareshwar: The Living Guardian of Kashi

Perched high above the river on the top tier of the steps, the Kedareshwar Temple is considered older than the current structure of the Vishwanath temple, having withstood historical invasions intact.

  • Svayambhu Shivalinga: The temple sanctum houses an uncarved rough natural stone lingam representing Lord Shiva in his mountain Kedarnath manifestation.
  • Gauri Kund Reservoir: At the base of the ghat steps sits the sacred Gauri Kund, a stone well filled with water believed to possess miraculous healing properties.
  • Puranic Guardian Role: According to local tradition, Lord Kedareshwar presides over the Southern zone (Kedar Khanda), protecting Kashi's spiritual equilibrium.

🛕 Sacred Mountain in Kashi

"To visit Kedareshwar on the Ganga is believed to grant the same eternal grace as the arduous Himalayan pilgrimage to Kedarnath."

Shravan Festivities & Shaivite Worship

Kedar Ghat is the epicentre of Shaivite devotional activity, especially during the auspicious monsoon month of Shravan (July–August).

  • Shravan Somwar Pilgrimages: Every Monday of Shravan, tens of thousands of devotees and Kanwariyas gather here at dawn to offer sacred Ganga water and bilva leaves.
  • Kedara Mahatmya Recitations: Brahmin scholars and monks gather along the riverfront stone pavilions to chant verses from the Kashi Khanda.
  • Maha Shivaratri Festivities: The temple and entire ghat facade are illuminated with oil lamps, accompanied by Vedic Rudrabhishekam rituals throughout the night.

🔱 Eternal Sanctity

The sacred waters fronting Kedar Ghat are revered for washing away karmic impediments before stepping into the inner sanctum.

Red-and-White Temple Stripes & River Steppes

Kedar Ghat is instantly recognizable from riverboats due to its iconic visual and architectural signatures.

  • Dravidian Vertical Stripes: The riverfront walls and upper temple towers are adorned with alternating vertical red and white stripes (*Namam* style), characteristic of South Indian temples.
  • Tiered Bathing Ghat Platforms: Wide sandstone platforms allow pilgrims to safely submerge and dry ceremonial clothes even during seasonal Ganga currents.
  • Steep Stone Flight of Stairs: A grand staircase rises directly from the waterline to the temple entrance gate, providing panoramic views across the Ganges.

🎨 Visual Landmark

The striking vertical red-and-white stripes make Kedar Ghat one of the most photographed riverfront facades along the Varanasi shoreline.

South Indian & Bengali Pilgrimage Sanctuary

For centuries, Kedar Ghat has been the welcoming harbor for pilgrims travelling from Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Bengal.

  • Dharamsalas & Mathas: The surrounding lanes (Kedar Galli) are populated with ancient traditional Choultries, South Indian Ashrams, and Kumaraswamy Matha.
  • Ritual Morning Ablutions: Pilgrims perform Tarpanam, Surya Namaskar, and ritual immersion in the Ganga before circumambulating the holy Gauri Kund.
  • Panchakroshi Yatra Halt: Kedar Ghat is an integral ritual station during the traditional 5-day circumambulation pilgrimage around the sacred circumference of Kashi.

🚶‍♂️ Cultural Crossroad

Kedar Ghat represents a timeless cultural bridge where South Indian temple rituals harmoniously blend with Varanasi's northern Ganga traditions.

💡 Historical & Cultural Facts About Kedar Ghat

  • Kedar Khanda Guardian: Ancient Varanasi was divided into three cosmological realms: Omkareshwar, Vishveshvara, and Kedareshwara.
  • Red & White Stripes: The alternating red and white stripes signify spiritual purification and protection according to Dravidian temple traditions.
  • Kumaraswamy Matha: Founded in the 16th century by Saint Kumaraswamy of Tamil Nadu, preserving centuries of South Indian Shaiva Siddhanta philosophy.
  • Svayambhu Lingam: The primary deity inside Kedareshwar is naturally formed, resembling a rough mountain boulder from the Garhwal Himalayas.
  • Gauri Kund Healing Waters: The small stone well at the steps is dedicated to Goddess Gauri and remains full year-round through natural subsoil aquifers.
  • Dawn Sun Salutations: Because of the eastern orientation across the wide Ganga, hundreds of yogis gather on Kedar's steps for morning Surya Namaskar.
