Trek Overview

The Border Skyline of Bengal

The Singalila Ridge forms the natural frontier between India and Nepal in the Darjeeling hills — a forested crest that carries trekkers to the highest point of West Bengal.

📍 Trek Location

The Singalila Ridge rises along the western edge of Darjeeling district in West Bengal, forming the natural frontier between India and Nepal. The entire trail runs inside Singalila National Park, a protected belt of Eastern Himalayan forest between roughly 2,400 m and 3,700 m, crowned by Sandakphu — the highest point of West Bengal.

🚩 Starting Point

The trailhead is Manebhanjan (2,150 m), a bustling border village about 26 km from Darjeeling, famous for its vintage British-era land rovers. The first climb leads through Chitrey and Meghma to the twin viewpoints of Tonglu and Tumling.

🏔️ Mountain Landscape

The ridge walk climbs through moss-draped temperate forest onto an open crest where the trail constantly weaves across the India–Nepal border. From the crest, a single unbroken skyline lines up Mount Everest, Lhotse, Makalu and the entire Kanchenjunga massif — famously profiled as the Sleeping Buddha.

📜 Border Heritage

The ridge has long been a trade and pilgrimage corridor between Nepal, Sikkim and the Darjeeling hills. Sherpa and Nepali villages such as Tumling, Gairibans and Kalipokhri still dot the trail with their lodges, monasteries and century-old land-rover transport culture.

Permits & Responsible Travel

Plan With Safety and Respect

🛂 Park Entry Permits

An entry permit for Singalila National Park is issued at the West Bengal Forest Department checkpost in Manebhanjan. Carry valid photo ID; foreign nationals should carry passport and visa details, and note that stepping into Nepal-side hamlets like Tumling requires standard border awareness even though no visa applies.

🥾 Fitness & Altitude

The gradients are gentle but the walking days are long — prepare for 5–7 hours daily on stone tracks. Sleep altitude stays below 3,700 m, yet hydrate well, ascend steadily, and descend immediately if altitude sickness symptoms appear.

🌿 Protect the Red Panda Forest

Singalila is a protected national park and one of India's key red panda habitats. Carry all plastic waste back out, keep quiet near wildlife, never feed animals, stay on the main track, and respect the sacred status of ponds like Kalipokhri and its endangered Himalayan newts.

🧭 Travel With Locals

Hire registered guides and land rovers from Manebhanjan, book village lodges in advance for autumn peaks, and check weather advisories — winter brings snow and ice above Kalipokhri, and monsoon months bring leeches and landslide-prone roads.

⚠️ Mountain Safety Notice

The Singalila Ridge Trek reaches 3,636 m and crosses a remote international border zone. This page is for educational exploration only — always rely on official forest department guidance, trained local trek leaders, and up-to-date conditions before planning any real-world trekking decisions.
