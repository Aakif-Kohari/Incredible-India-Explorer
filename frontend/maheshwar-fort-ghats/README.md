# 🏰 Maheshwar Fort Ghats: Stone Architecture Along the Narmada

## Overview
A comprehensive explorer for **Maheshwar Fort Ghats** in Madhya Pradesh, showcasing one of India's most remarkable examples of integrated fort and riverfront architecture. Built primarily under Rajmata Ahilyabai Holkar in the 18th century, the complex demonstrates how fort defense and sacred riverfront can form a unified architectural ensemble along the sacred Narmada River.
