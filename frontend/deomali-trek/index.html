Deomali Peak Trek ଦେଓମାଳୀ — The Crown Peak of Odisha (Koraput)

Soaring 1,672 meters above sea level in the Eastern Ghats of Koraput, Deomali is the highest mountain peak in Odisha. Experience rolling velvet grasslands, morning seas of clouds, indigenous tribal heritage, and high-altitude Arabica coffee hills.

How to Reach & Local Information

🚆 Transport Options

  • Nearest Railhead: Koraput / Jeypore / Damanjodi station (~45 to 65 km).
  • Nearest Airport: Jeypore Airport (~70 km) / Visakhapatnam Airport (~190 km).
  • By Road: Taxis & buses available from Koraput or Semiliguda to Pottangi / Deomali base.

🏡 Eco-Stay & Local Experiences

  • OTDC Eco-Cottages: OTDC operates nature camp cottages at Deomali foothills.
  • Kunduli Friday Market: Visit the largest weekly tribal market in South Odisha.
  • Koraput Coffee: Sample freshly roasted local Arabica coffee at Koraput cafes.
