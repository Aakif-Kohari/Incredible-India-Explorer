Sakhya Sagar Wetland Explorer

Discover the scenic reservoir built by the Scindias of Gwalior in 1918, now a Ramsar Wetland of International Importance inside Madhav National Park — home to basking mugger crocodiles, migratory waterfowl, and a fragile, actively managed ecosystem.
