Thane Creek Wetland Explorer

Discover the "Sea of Pink" wedged between Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and Thane. A tidal mangrove creek that became Mumbai's first Ramsar Wetland of International Importance, Thane Creek turns pink every winter as tens of thousands of flamingos arrive to feed.
