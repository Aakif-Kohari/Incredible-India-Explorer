🔍 Debunking Popular Myths vs Verified History

Distinguishing verified primary Mughal archival records from unverified popular folklore and modern legends.

Myth: Artisans' Hands Mutilated DISPUTED / UNFOUNDED MYTH

Historical Verdict: Zero contemporary 17th-century Mughal primary sources (e.g. Badshahnama) support this claim. Craftsmen received pensions and built Old Delhi afterward.

Myth: Black Taj Mahal across Yamuna DISPUTED / LEGENDARY MYTH

Historical Verdict: ASI excavations at Mahtab Bagh proved the "black marble" ruins were dark reflections in the garden basin and blackened white marble fragments.

Myth: Pre-existing Ancient Temple Site HISTORICALLY DEBUNKED

Historical Verdict: Royal imperial Farman land deeds confirm Shah Jahan traded four mansions with Raja Jai Singh of Amber to acquire the riverfront plot.
