Haldiram's (India's Snack & Food Brand)

Discover the journey of Haldiram's — from Ganga Bishan Agarwal's small bhujia shop in Bikaner, Rajasthan, to one of India's most recognized namkeen, sweets, and restaurant brands, carrying traditional flavors to households and dining tables worldwide.

Origin & Founder History

Haldiram's traces its roots to Ganga Bishan Agarwal, affectionately nicknamed "Haldiram", who began selling a distinctively spiced bhujia from a small shop in Bikaner, Rajasthan, in 1937. Built on a family tradition of sweet-making, the business grew from a single regional counter into a name synonymous with Indian namkeen and mithai, later expanding through separate family-run branches based in Nagpur and Delhi that each built the brand into a major national and international food business.
