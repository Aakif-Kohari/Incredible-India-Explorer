🏙️ Port Blair

Port Blair is the capital of the Andaman & Nicobar Islands and the main gateway for visitors arriving by air or sea. Often called "Mini India" for the diversity of communities settled there, the city blends colonial-era landmarks with everyday island life — markets, seafood, and a laid-back coastal pace.

Direct flights connect Port Blair to Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru and Mumbai, while passenger ships from Kolkata and Chennai take 3–4 days.

⛓️ Cellular Jail

Also known as Kala Pani, the Cellular Jail is a colonial-era prison built in 1896 that once held Indian freedom fighters in solitary confinement. Its three-storeyed building originally had 698 cells arranged in a radiating, starfish-like layout, designed so no prisoner could see or speak with another.

Today it stands as a National Memorial and museum, with an evening Light & Sound Show retelling the story of India's freedom struggle at the site itself.

  • Open 9 AM – 5 PM, all days except national holidays
  • Evening Light & Sound Show
  • Adjacent museum galleries on the freedom movement

🐦 Chidiya Tapu

Known as "Bird Island," Chidiya Tapu lies about 25 km south of Port Blair and is renowned as one of the best sunset viewpoints in the Andamans, alongside nature trails and mangrove boardwalks.

  • Home to over 46 species of birds
  • Munda Pahad viewpoint, also called Suicide Point, for panoramic sunsets
  • Chidiya Tapu Biological Park for local flora and fauna

🏛️ Ross Island

Officially renamed Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Dweep, Ross Island was the British administrative headquarters for the entire Andaman & Nicobar Islands before India's independence. Today its colonial-era buildings stand in atmospheric ruin, wrapped in the roots of giant banyan and rubber trees.

  • Ruins of the former British Chief Commissioner's residence, church and bakery
  • Resident deer and peacocks roaming the grounds
  • Reachable by a short boat ride from Port Blair, often combined with North Bay Island

🏺 Museums

Port Blair is home to several museums that together give a rounded picture of the islands' history, ecology and indigenous cultures.

  • Anthropological Museum — artifacts, tools and photographs of the islands' indigenous tribes
  • Samudrika Naval Marine Museum — marine life, shells and island geography
  • Fisheries (Marine) Museum — aquatic species of the surrounding seas
  • Cellular Jail Museum — exhibits on the freedom struggle

🧭 Tourism

South Andaman offers a mix of history, beaches and water sports. Along with Port Blair's core sights, nearby attractions include Corbyn's Cove Beach, the Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park, Chatham Saw Mill (Asia's oldest sawmill), and Wandoor Beach.

The best time to visit is between October and May, with January to April offering the most reliable weather for water sports such as snorkelling, scuba diving and sea walking.
