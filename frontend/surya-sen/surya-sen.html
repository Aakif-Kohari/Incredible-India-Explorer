01

The Masterda

Surya Sen, popularly known as Masterda, was a teacher and revolutionary leader from Chittagong.

He played a central role in organising revolutionary activities against British colonial rule in the Chittagong region.

His most famous action was the Chittagong Armoury Raid of April 1930, which attempted to disrupt British authority and inspire a wider uprising.
