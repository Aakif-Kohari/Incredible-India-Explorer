Khudiram Bose

The Young Martyr of India

A tale of unparalleled courage and sacrifice in the revolutionary movement.

Biography

Born on December 3, 1889, in Habibpur village of Midnapore district in Bengal, Khudiram Bose grew up in an environment charged with anti-British sentiment following the 1905 partition of Bengal.

He was drawn to revolutionary activities at a very young age and became a volunteer in the Anushilan Samiti, a prominent revolutionary group in Bengal. His dedication and fearlessness quickly made him a notable figure among young revolutionaries.

Bose is most remembered for his role in the Muzaffarpur bombing attempt in 1908, aimed at assassinating the Chief Presidency Magistrate Douglas Kingsford, who was known for his harsh judgments against Indian nationalists.
