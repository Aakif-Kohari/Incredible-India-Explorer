Araku Valley Trek — Andhra Pradesh

Discover the mystic Eastern Ghats, lush green valleys, dense forests, and vibrant tribal heritage.

Trek Profile & Details

  • Location: Alluri Sitharama Raju District, Eastern Ghats, Andhra Pradesh
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Distance & Duration: 12 km (approx. 2 Days)
  • Best Season: September to March (Post-monsoon & Winter)
  • Starting Point: Galikonda Viewpoint / Katiki Village

Natural Features & Biodiversity

Valley Landscape

Characterized by rolling hills, misty tablelands, and deep coffee plantations nestled in the Eastern Ghats.

Forests & Biodiversity

Rich deciduous and semi-evergreen forests housing endemic flora, rare medicinal herbs, and diverse avifauna.

Local Culture & Heritage

Experience the authentic tribal lifestyle of indigenous communities such as the Bagata, Kondadora, and Valmiki tribes. Witness traditional folk dances like Dhimsa and learn about organic coffee cultivation and local handicrafts.

Nearby Attractions

  • Borra Caves (Million-year-old limestone formations)
  • Katiki Waterfalls
  • Padmapuram Botanical Gardens
  • Coffee Museum
