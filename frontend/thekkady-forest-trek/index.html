Forest Canopy & Biological Diversity

The Kumily–Thekkady Forest Trek, also famous as Border Hiking or Nature Walk, is organized inside the buffer zone of the prestigious Periyar Tiger Reserve. Spanning over 925 square kilometers of the southern Western Ghats, this tropical forest ecosystem is celebrated worldwide for its high concentration of endemic fauna and flora.

The trail passes under towering semi-evergreen canopies, giant teak groves, and sprawling bamboo thickets. Trekking here offers an unparalleled chance to observe wildlife in their natural habitat, including herds of Asian Elephants grazing along the grassy banks of Periyar Lake, Sambar Deer, Wild Boars, Otters, Nilgiri Langurs, and the colorful Malabar Giant Squirrel. It is also a birdwatcher's paradise, hosting the Great Indian Hornbill, Malabar Grey Hornbill, and diverse tropical butterflies.

💡 Quick Trek Tips

  • Local Guides: Treks are only permitted when accompanied by an authorized forest guide. Never attempt to venture into the forest alone.
  • Leeches: Post-monsoon trails are full of forest leeches. Wear leech socks provided by the reserve office or spray salt/tobacco.
  • Silence: Loud noises scare animals away. Maintain absolute silence and walk in a single file line.
  • Advance Booking: Guided forest walks and border hiking programs have limited slots daily. Book online via the Periyar Reserve portal.

Trek Route & Day-wise Highlights

The standard border hike loop traces reservoir shorelines, deciduous buffers, and high border ridges before checking out at the gate.

Responsible Trekking & Eco-Guidelines

Periyar Tiger Reserve is a strictly protected biodiversity zone. Trekkers are expected to adhere to standard wilderness codes of conduct to ensure minimal ecological footprint:

  • Plastic-Free Sanctuary: Single-use plastic bottles, snack wrappers, and polythene carry bags are strictly prohibited. Forest guards inspect bags at checkposts.
  • Acoustic Protection: Refrain from playing music on speakers, shouting, or whistling. Let the sounds of the forest prevail.
  • Muted Dress Code: Wear earth-toned colors (khaki, forest green, olive, brown) to blend with the surroundings. Avoid bright red, yellow, or white.
  • Zero-Trace Policy: Pack back all trash, including organic waste like fruit peels, to prevent foreign seeds from spreading inside the reserve.
  • Wildlife Boundaries: Maintain a safe distance of at least 50 meters from any spotted mammal. Do not block their paths or try to feed them.

♻️ Eco Tourism Award

Periyar Tiger Reserve has received the **UN-sponsored India Biodiversity Award** for its sustainable community-based eco-tourism initiatives, transforming former forest poachers into certified conservation guides.

History & Wildlife Conservation

From a royal game reserve under the Travancore Kingdom to one of India's premier tiger protection sanctuaries, Periyar's conservation history spans over a century.
