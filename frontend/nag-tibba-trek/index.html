Nag Tibba: The Serpent's Peak

Uttarakhand's highest peak in the Nag Tibba Range at 3,022 meters. A perfect introduction to Himalayan trekking with breathtaking views of Bandarpoonch, Swargarohini, and Gangotri ranges.

The Perfect Himalayan Introduction

Nag Tibba, meaning "Serpent's Peak," is the highest peak in the Nag Tibba Range of the Garhwal Himalayas in Uttarakhand. Standing at 3,022 meters (9,915 feet), it offers a perfect introduction to Himalayan trekking for beginners while rewarding experienced trekkers with stunning panoramic views.

Trek Essentials

Location: Tehri Garhwal District, Uttarakhand
Base Camp: Pantwari Village (1,500m)
Nearest Town: Mussoorie (55 km), Dehradun (90 km)
Best Season: March to June, September to November
Trek Type: Round trip (same route up and down)
Maximum Altitude: 3,022m at Nag Tibba Summit
Total Distance: 16 km round trip (8 km one way)

The trek is named after the local deity Nag Devta (Snake God), whose temple stands near the summit. The dense forests of oak, deodar, and rhododendron provide a magical canopy throughout the trail, especially vibrant during spring when rhododendrons bloom in shades of red and pink.

Trail Breakdown

The Nag Tibba trek follows a well-defined trail through diverse landscapes, from subtropical forests to alpine meadows. The gradual ascent makes it accessible to first-time trekkers while offering rewarding views at every turn.

Panoramic Viewpoints

The trek rewards hikers with increasingly spectacular views as altitude increases. From the summit, on clear days, you can see major Himalayan peaks spanning across Uttarakhand and into Tibet.

Campsites & Accommodation

The trek offers several camping options, from basic tent sites to more established camps with facilities. Most trekkers complete it in 2 days with one night's camping.
