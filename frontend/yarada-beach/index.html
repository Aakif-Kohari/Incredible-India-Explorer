Yarada Beach

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

A spectacular, secluded coastal gem cradled on three sides by the emerald-green crags of the Dolphin's Nose hill range and bordered on the fourth by the powerful surf of the Bay of Bengal.

🌐 Geographical Profile & Landscape Architecture

  • Location: Behind the Dolphin’s Nose Hill Range, approximately 15 kilometres south of Visakhapatnam City Centre.
  • The Surrounding Hills: The beach is uniquely isolated from city noises by towering cliffs covered in thick tropical vegetation and Banana/Coconut plantations. The road leading down the hill range offers a breathtaking panoramic overview of the ocean meeting the valley.
  • Coastal Features: A sweeping, deep golden sand beach stretching across a secluded bay. Because the shoreline is protected by natural rock projections and cliff formations, it maintains a clean, pristine environment that feels entirely cut off from urban pollution.

🏄 Major Recreational Activities & Natural Attractions

  • Landscape Photography: The meeting point of steep, rocky green hills and golden sand beaches makes it one of the premier spots for landscape photography on India's eastern coast.
  • Hill Trekking & Scenic Driving: The drive over the Dolphin's Nose hill ridge features sharp hairpin bends and excellent cliffside viewpoints overlooking the blue horizon.
  • Beach Lounging & Picnics: The lush coconut groves bordering the sand provide plenty of cool, natural shade, making it a favorite spot for quiet family retreats.
  • Swimming Warning: Swimming is highly dangerous and strongly discouraged. The underwater shelf has a steep drop-off close to the shore, creating powerful undertows and unpredictable rip currents. Wading past ankle depth should be avoided.

🗺️ Nearby Places Worth Visiting

  • Dolphin’s Nose Lighthouse: Perched on the massive rocky headland facing the sea, this powerful military lighthouse offers unmatched birds-eye views of the Vizag port and shipping channels.
  • Ross Hill Church, Ishak Madina Dargah, & Venkateswara Temple: Three distinct shrines situated across three adjacent hilltops overlooking the harbor entrance, representing historical multi-cultural harmony.
  • Scindia Lighthouse & Vizag Port: Located nearby, this historical zone lets travelers watch massive merchant ships and naval vessels move through narrow rock channels into the inner harbor.
