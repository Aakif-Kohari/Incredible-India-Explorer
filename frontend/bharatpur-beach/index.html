Bharatpur Beach

Neil Island's Calm Turquoise Lagoon & Family-Friendly Shore

Tucked on the western coast of Neil Island — renamed Shaheed Dweep in 2018 — Bharatpur Beach is one of the Andamans' most beloved family beaches. Its shallow, glass-clear lagoon shelves gently into the sea, making it wonderfully safe for swimming, while fringing coral reefs just offshore teem with colourful marine life visible even at low tide.

Beach Type Shallow Coral Lagoon
Best Season October – May
Access Ferry from Port Blair
Vibe Family-friendly & Calm

📍 Location & Geographical Profile

Bharatpur Beach lies on the western shore of Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep), a small island in the Ritchie's Archipelago of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, roughly 40 kilometres northeast of Port Blair. The beach sits about 2 kilometres from the island's main jetty and settlement of Neil Kendra, and is reached by a short, scenic road through coconut groves.

Administrative Region Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep), South Andaman district, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, India
Geographical Coordinates 11.8300° N, 93.0300° E (island); Bharatpur Beach on the western coast
Coastline Terrain Wide stretch of white sand with a very gentle slope into a shallow, calm turquoise lagoon.
Offshore Feature Fringing coral reef flats exposed at low tide, ideal for reef walking and viewing.
Getting There Ferry from Port Blair (~2 hrs) or Swaraj Dweep / Havelock (~1 hr); then ~2 km from the jetty.

🏖️ Beach Overview & Landscape

  • Shallow Turquoise Lagoon: The water stays shallow for a long way out, creating a huge natural paddling pool that is exceptionally safe for children and weak swimmers.
  • Powdery White Sand: Soft, fine sand lines the shore, dotted with coral fragments and shells washed up by the gentle surf.
  • Palm-Fringed Shore: Coconut palms and tropical vegetation shade the backshore, offering natural picnic spots and cool resting places.
  • Low-Tide Coral Flats: At low tide, the reef flats emerge as shallow pools teeming with starfish, sea cucumbers and small reef fish — a natural aquarium at your feet.

🐠 Marine Environment

  • Fringing Coral Reefs: Healthy hard-coral communities grow just offshore, part of the rich reef system of the Ritchie's Archipelago near the Rani Jhansi Marine National Park.
  • Colourful Reef Fish: Snorkelers can spot damselfish, parrotfish, butterflyfish and angelfish weaving through the coral heads.
  • Gentle Giants of the Shallows: Sea turtles occasionally cruise the lagoon, and sea cucumbers, starfish and hermit crabs are common in the low-tide pools.
  • Conservation Etiquette: Visitors are urged not to stand on or break corals, to wear reef-safe sunscreen, and to take all litter back — the reef is a protected, living ecosystem.

Things to Do at Bharatpur Beach

Bharatpur is the activity hub of Neil Island, offering gentle, reef-friendly experiences for all ages:

  • Swimming & Wading: The shallow, calm lagoon is one of the safest swimming beaches in the Andamans — ideal for families with young children.
  • Snorkeling: Rent a mask and fins and drift over the coral gardens just a short swim from shore to meet the reef's colourful residents.
  • Glass-Bottom Boat Rides: Take a boat with a glass viewing panel to cruise over the reef without getting wet — perfect for non-swimmers and kids.
  • Low-Tide Reef Walking: At low tide, walk the exposed reef flats with a guide to spot starfish, sea cucumbers and tiny fish in the pools.
  • Beachcombing & Photography: Stroll the white sands at sunrise or sunset for shell hunting, birdwatching and postcard-perfect photographs.

Nearby Attractions on Neil Island

  • Natural Bridge (Howrah Bridge): A dramatic natural rock arch carved by the sea on the island's coast, best visited at low tide — a short drive from Bharatpur.
  • Laxmanpur Beach: The island's famous sunset point, where the sun sinks directly into the sea over a wide, shallow shelf.
  • Sitapur Beach: The eastern shore's sunrise viewpoint, with a long stretch of white sand and gentle morning light.
  • Neil Kendra & Local Market: The island's main settlement, with shops, eateries, and the jetty where ferries from Port Blair and Swaraj Dweep arrive.
  • Shaheed Dweep Cycling Trails: Rent a bicycle or scooter to explore the island's quiet roads through coconut and banana plantations.

Essential Visitor Guidelines & Practical Info

  • Best Time to Visit: October to May offers calm seas and clear skies; November to April is the peak season with the most reliable ferry connections.
  • Monsoon Note (June–September): Heavy rains and rough seas can suspend ferry services; swimming is not advised during stormy conditions.
  • Entry & Facilities: A small entry ticket is collected at the beach; basic changing rooms, washrooms and a few snack stalls are available near the entrance.
  • Safety: Lifeguards patrol the swimming zone; swim within the flagged area and supervise children at all times.
  • Eco Code: Do not touch or stand on corals, avoid single-use plastics, and carry out everything you bring in.
