# 🏥 Rajkumari Amrit Kaur: Architect of Public Health

## Overview
A comprehensive historical profile of **Rajkumari Amrit Kaur** (1889-1964), freedom fighter, social reformer, and India's first Health Minister. This explorer documents her remarkable journey from royal privilege to dedicated national service, and her pivotal role in building India's public health system.

## Key Contributions
- First Health Minister of independent India (1947-1957)
- Established AIIMS New Delhi (1956)
- Founded Indian Nursing Council
- Led WHO initiatives as President of World Health Assembly
- Championed women's rights and education
- Built India's public health infrastructure
