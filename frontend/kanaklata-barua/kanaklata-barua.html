Kanaklata Barua

A story of courage, youth, and resistance during India's freedom struggle.

01

A Young Voice of Resistance

Kanaklata Barua was a young freedom fighter from Assam who became a symbol of courage during the Quit India Movement of 1942.

At only 17 years old, she led a group of volunteers determined to hoist the Indian national flag at the British-controlled Gohpur police station.

Her story reminds us that age does not determine the strength of one's courage or convictions.

The Call for Freedom

In 1942, the Quit India Movement called for an end to British rule. Across Assam, young people joined the movement and became involved in demonstrations and volunteer groups.

02 / 04

A Young Volunteer

Kanaklata became associated with the Mrityu Bahini, a volunteer group connected with the resistance movement. Despite her young age, she showed determination to participate in the struggle.

03 / 04

The Flag March

On 20 September 1942, Kanaklata led volunteers towards the Gohpur police station carrying the national flag. The procession faced resistance from the authorities.

04 / 04

A Legacy That Remains

Kanaklata's sacrifice became part of Assam's memory of the independence movement. Her story continues to inspire young people to understand the courage of India's freedom fighters.
