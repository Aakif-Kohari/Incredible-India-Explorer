Pin Parvati Pass Trek Parvati to Pin Valley — Glacial High Pass Traverse (5,319m)

One of India's most challenging and prestigious mountain expeditions. Traverse 110 km from the lush green forests of Kullu's Parvati Valley, past Mantalai Lake, across crevassed glaciers, over the 5,319m pass to emerge into the barren cold desert of Spiti's Pin Valley.

Discover Pin Parvati Pass Trek

The Pin Parvati Pass Trek is an extraordinary trans-Himalayan traverse connecting the lush, forest-draped Parvati Valley in Kullu with the stark, arid cold desert of the Pin Valley in Spiti. Standing at a formidable 5,319 meters, this high pass demands supreme physical endurance and mountaineering skills.

The trek crosses a massive glacier area filled with hidden crevasses, roaring rivers, and high moraine slopes. Trekkers experience a complete transformation in landscape and culture, beginning with the Hindu-influenced forest hamlets of Kullu and finishing at the Tibetan-Buddhist mud villages of Spiti.

Major Attractions & Highlights

💡 Quick Trek Tips

  • Permit Requirements: Mandatory inner line and forest permits must be registered at Kaza (Spiti) or Kullu Forest offices.
  • Guide Compulsory: Doing this trek without an experienced certified mountain guide is highly dangerous. Roping is required on glaciers.
  • River Crossings: Parvati River crossings are notorious. Make sure to cross early in the morning when water levels are low.
  • Best Time: July to September, when Spiti roads are open and high passes are relatively free of winter blockades.

Trek Route & Day-wise Highlights

The standard traverse starts from Barsheni in Kullu, climbs through Parvati gorges, crosses the pass glacier, and descends to Mud village in Spiti.

🚨 Compulsory Safety & Survival Guidelines

Pin Parvati Pass is considered an extreme expedition. The combination of high altitude (5,319m), glacial crevasses, volatile weather, and remoteness creates serious survival hazards. Please adhere strictly to these rules:

1. High Altitude & AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness)

Since the trek climbs from 2,000m to 5,319m, AMS, HAPE, and HACE are major risks. Carry acetazolamide (Diamox), climb gradually (incorporating acclimatization days at Mantalai Lake), and descend immediately if severe headache, dizziness, or breathing trouble occurs.

2. Glacier Safety & Roping Protocols

The pass has a massive crevassed glacier slope. Hidden crevasses under snow bridges are common. **Roping up** with other team members and using crampons/microspikes is compulsory when crossing ice fields.

3. River Crossing Dangers

Mountain rivers in Parvati valley are highly turbulent. Early morning crossings (before 8 AM) are compulsory, as meltwater raises river levels significantly later in the day. Use rope supports and guide instructions.

⚠️ Emergency Evacuation

There is no cellular connectivity beyond Kheerganga and Mud. Carry a satellite phone or notify local police/forest offices at Barsheni and Kaza before departing. The nearest rescue stations are at Joshimath, Manali, and Kaza.
