01

Who Was Kanhu Murmu?

Kanhu Murmu was one of the principal leaders of the Santhal Hul of 1855–56.

Along with his brother Sidhu Murmu and other leaders, Kanhu became an important figure in the Santhal resistance against exploitative systems and colonial authority.

The Hul, meaning rebellion or uprising, brought together large numbers of Santhal people in a powerful challenge to oppression.

1855: The Hul Begins

In June 1855, the Santhal uprising gained momentum around Bhognadih. Sidhu and Kanhu Murmu emerged as important leaders of the movement.

04 / 05

A Wider Uprising

The movement spread across parts of the Santhal region. The rebels challenged colonial institutions and those they regarded as agents of exploitation.

A Legacy of Resistance

The uprising was eventually suppressed, but the Santhal Hul remains an important part of the history of resistance against colonial rule and exploitation.
