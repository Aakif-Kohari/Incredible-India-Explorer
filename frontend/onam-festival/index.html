Onam: Kerala's Harvest Festival

Welcome the spirit of King Mahabali and celebrate the vibrant floral carpets, grand feasts, and snake boat races of God's Own Country.

The Legend of Mahabali

Onam celebrates the annual homecoming of the benevolent Asura King Mahabali. According to Hindu mythology, Mahabali's reign was a golden era of equality, prosperity, and happiness. The gods, feeling threatened by his popularity, requested Lord Vishnu to intervene.

Vamana Avatar

Vishnu descended as Vamana, a dwarf Brahmin, and asked Mahabali for three paces of land. When granted, Vamana grew to cosmic proportions, covering the earth and sky in two steps. For the third step, the generous Mahabali offered his own head, pushing him into the netherworld but granting him the boon to visit his people once a year—this day is Onam.

The Grand Onam Sadya

The Onam Sadya is a magnificent vegetarian feast served on a fresh banana leaf. It consists of over 26 dishes, each with a specific placement and order of consumption.
