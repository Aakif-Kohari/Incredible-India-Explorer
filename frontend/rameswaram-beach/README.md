# Rameswaram Beach & Pamban Island Explorer

## Overview
An interactive destination profile showcasing **Rameswaram Beach (Agni Theertham)** in Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu — where the waters of the Bay of Bengal meet the Gulf of Mannar.

## Key Features & Highlights
- **Sacred Shores & Char Dham Lore:** Agni Theertham, Ramanathaswamy Temple, and the story of Ram Setu.
- **Interactive 22 Sacred Wells (Theerthams) Explorer:** Filterable guide for all 22 sweet-water wells within the temple complex.
- **Pamban Sea Bridge & Dhanushkodi Ghost Town:** Exploration of India's historic cantilever rail bridge and the cyclone ruins of Dhanushkodi.
- **Curated Multi-Day Itineraries:** 1-Day Express Pilgrimage, 2-Day Pamban Island Explorer, and 3-Day Gulf of Mannar Circuit.
- **Interactive Leaflet Map:** Landmark markers across Pamban Island with directions links.
- **Visual Image Gallery & Modal Lightbox:** Category filtering and keyboard-navigable lightbox (`Left`/`Right` arrow keys, `Esc`).
- **Web Audio API Ambient Shore Soundscape:** Zero-dependency, offline-friendly ambient sea waves generator.
- **Dual-Theme Support:** Harmonized Dark and Light modes.
- **Full SEO & Schema.org Structured Data:** Semantic HTML5 and JSON-LD `@type: "Beach"` markup.
