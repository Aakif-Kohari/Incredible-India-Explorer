Partition of India, 1947 A Factual Overview

A dignified, non-graphic historical record documenting the political context, boundary demarcation, migration scale, and subsequent nation-building of 1947.

⚖️

Editorial Note & Historical Framing

This presentation offers a factual, neutral, and respectful overview of the 1947 Partition of British India, focusing on political decisions, verified demographic scale, and nation-building aftermath. Content is compiled from peer-reviewed historical literature and official census archives. In accordance with strict educational guidelines, graphic depictions of violence are omitted to maintain an objective, dignified focus on human history and resilience.

Demographic & Migration Scale (Sourced Data)

Verified figures on population movements during the 1947 demarcation.

👥 14–15 Million Total Displaced Population

Estimated total cross-border movement across Punjab, Bengal, and Sindh.

🇮🇳 7.2 Million Migration to India

5.4M from West Pakistan & 1.8M from East Pakistan (Census of India 1951).

🇵🇰 7.2 Million Migration to Pakistan

7.2M from East Punjab, Delhi, and UP (Government of Pakistan Reports).

Constitutional Integration (1950)

The Constitution of India came into effect on January 26, 1950, guaranteeing secular democracy, fundamental rights, and equal citizenship regardless of religion or origin.

Refugee Townships & Rehabilitation

New model townships like Faridabad, Nilokheri, and Ulhasnagar were constructed to house displaced families and foster industrial & handicraft livelihoods.
