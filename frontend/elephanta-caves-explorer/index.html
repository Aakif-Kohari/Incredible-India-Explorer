Elephanta Caves Explorer

A rock-cut island temple in Mumbai Harbour, near Mumbai, Maharashtra — home to the monumental Trimurti and some of India's finest Shaivite sculpture.

History

A site shaped by ancient devotion, colonial conflict, and modern restoration.

Island Heritage

Elephanta Island, locally called Gharapuri — "the city of caves" — sits about 10 km east of Mumbai's Gateway of India, reached by a roughly one-hour ferry across the harbour.

Rock-Cut Architecture

Cave 1, the Great Cave, is carved entirely from solid basalt rock and remains the site's architectural centerpiece.

Trimurti

The monumental three-faced Shiva bust is the single most celebrated sculpture at Elephanta.

Shiva Sculptures

Beyond the Trimurti, Cave 1 holds a rich gallery of large relief panels depicting Shiva's many forms and legends.

UNESCO Heritage

Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1987, recognized for its outstanding contribution to Indian rock-cut art.
