# Maitreyi — The Philosopher Who Chose Knowledge Over Material Wealth

## Overview
An interactive historical and philosophical exploration of **Maitreyi (मैत्रेयी)** — the esteemed Vedic scholar, philosopher, and *brahmavādinī* from the **Brihadaranyaka Upanishad** (Chapters 2.4 and 4.5), renowned for her immortal inquiry into the nature of the Self (*Ātman*) and her deliberate rejection of material riches (*vitta*) in pursuit of immortality (*amṛtatva*).

## Key Features & Highlights
- **Sacred Dialogue Reconstruction:** Step-by-step presentation of the farewell conversation between Maitreyi and sage Yajnavalkya as he prepares to transition from householder life (*Gṛhastha*) into wandering renunciation (*Sannyāsa*).
- **Primary Sanskrit Verses & Epistemology:** Direct Sanskrit passages in Devanagari with transliteration and translation:
  - *येनाहं नामृता स्यां किमहं तेन कुर्याम्* (*"What should I do with that by which I do not become immortal?"* — Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 2.4.3)
  - *न वा अरे पत्युः कामाय पतिः प्रियो भवति, आत्मनस्तु कामाय पतिः प्रियो भवति* (*"Not for the sake of the husband is the husband dear, but for the sake of the Self is the husband dear..."* — Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 2.4.5)
- **The Threefold Method of Realisation (*Sādhana-Trayam*):** Interactive breakdown of *Śravaṇa* (hearing/study), *Manana* (dialectical reflection), and *Nididhyāsana* (profound contemplative meditation).
- **Interactive Knowledge Check Quiz:** Real-time feedback testing comprehension of Maitreyi's core philosophical premise.
- **Historiographical Callout:** Clear demarcation between canonical Upanishadic source evidence and later commentarial/devotional traditions (e.g. Sureshvara's *Vārtika*, Adi Shankara's *Bhāṣya*).
- **Women in Vedic Philosophy Survey:** Contextual exploration comparing Maitreyi, Gargi Vachaknavi, the *Brahmavādinī* tradition, and the modern legacy of Maitreyi College (University of Delhi).
- **Full SEO & Schema.org Structured Data:** Semantic HTML5 with `@type: "Article"` and `@type: "Person"` JSON-LD markup.
