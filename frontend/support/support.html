Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the Interactive Map?

Navigate to the Interactive Map section on the Home page. You can hover over any state to see quick information, or click on a state to open a detailed overlay about its culture, capital, and famous food.

Is Incredible India Explorer free to use?

Yes! The core platform, including the interactive map, festival guides, and cuisine explorer, is completely free. We also offer a Premium tier with 360° VR Tours and advanced planning tools.

How does the Route Planner work?

The Route Planner allows you to select a starting point and destination. It then highlights key heritage sites, cultural landmarks, and hidden gems along your route, providing estimated travel times and recommendations.

How do I switch to Light Mode?

You can click the ☀️ icon in the top right corner of the navigation bar to toggle between dark and light themes. Your preference will be saved for future visits.
