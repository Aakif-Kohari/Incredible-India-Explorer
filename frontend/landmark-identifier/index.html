      Privacy & Technology

      How We Protect Your Privacy

      🔒 Browser-Based Processing

      Your photos are analyzed entirely in your browser using client-side AI. They are never uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

      💾 Local Storage Only

      Search history and preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. You can clear them anytime using the "Clear History" button.

      TensorFlow.js Powered

      We use TensorFlow.js for on-device image recognition, ensuring fast, private, and offline-capable landmark identification.
