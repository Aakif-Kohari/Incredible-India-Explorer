Chembra Peak Trek ചെമ്പ്ര കൊടുമുടി — Wayanad, Kerala

Rising 2,100 meters above sea level near Meppadi in Wayanad, Chembra Peak is the highest peak in the district. Renowned for its misty tea plantation foothills, dense Shola rainforests, and the world-famous natural heart-shaped lake (Hridayathadam), Chembra offers one of Southern India's most breathtaking mountain experiences.

Mountain & Forest Environment

Chembra Peak forms a majestic backdrop to the tea-growing region of Wayanad. The trek ascends through three distinct ecological zones: rolling tea plantation gardens, dense tropical evergreen rainforests, and high-altitude montane Shola grasslands.

🌲

Shola & Tropical Rainforests

The mid-elevation trail passes through rich Shola forest patches. These stunted tropical montane forests house moss-draped evergreen trees, endemic ferns, orchid species, and birdlife unique to the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve.

🌾

Montane Grassland Ridges

Above the forest line, expansive montane grasslands cover the mountain slopes. These windswept grassy ridges provide unobstructed views across Wayanad, Nilgiri hills border, and distant Kozhikode mountain passes.

Natural Wonder of Wayanad

Hridayathadam — Heart-Shaped Lake

Located halfway up the mountain ridge at an altitude of approximately 1,500 meters (4,920 ft), the heart-shaped lake is Chembra's primary attraction. Known locally in Malayalam as Hridayathadam (or Hridaya Saras), this natural waterbody has maintained its distinctive heart shape since time immemorial.

Hydrological Feature

💧 Perennial Water Body

Remarkably, the lake has never dried up in recorded history, maintaining its water level even during hot summer months. It receives mist precipitation and underground seepage from the surrounding Shola ridge.

Eco-Protection Rules

🛡️ Forest Conservation Regulations

To preserve the pristine water purity and protect surrounding flora, the Kerala Forest Department strictly prohibits stepping into or swimming in the lake. Trekkers view and photograph the lake from surrounding grassy embankments.

Trail Progression

Step-by-Step Route Breakdown

The trek begins at the VSS Meppadi Forest Checkpost. Trekkers walk through tea gardens, enter the forest zone, pass the watchtower, and climb the grassy slopes to the heart-shaped lake.
