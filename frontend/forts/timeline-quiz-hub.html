Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Hill Fort? ▼
A hill fort is a fortified refuge or defended settlement built upon an elevated terrain or a steep hill. These were particularly favored by the Marathas and Rajputs due to the natural defensive advantage the terrain provided against enemy sieges.
How are Sea Forts different? ▼
Sea forts (or coastal forts) are built on islands or directly along the coastline. They were designed specifically to defend against naval bombardments and control maritime trade routes. Examples include Sindhudurg, Diu Fort, and Murud-Janjira.
Which Indian forts are UNESCO World Heritage sites? ▼
Notable UNESCO World Heritage forts include the Hill Forts of Rajasthan (Chittorgarh, Kumbhalgarh, Ranthambore, Gagron, Amber, Jaisalmer), the Red Fort Complex in Delhi, and Agra Fort.
