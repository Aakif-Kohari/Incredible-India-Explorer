📜 Tournament History

Wrestling contests in India long predate modern sport organisation. Royal courts and village akharas staged kushti bouts for centuries, and Rajarshi Shahu Maharaj of Kolhapur gave the tradition a grand permanent arena in the early 1900s.

The modern era began when the Wrestling Federation of India (WFI) was constituted on 27 January 1967. The WFI now organises the annual Senior, Junior, and Sub-Junior National Wrestling Championships under United World Wrestling rules, drawing thousands of wrestlers from state affiliates, the Services, and the Railways.

National champions form the pool from which India's international teams are selected. That pipeline has produced every one of India's eight Olympic wrestling medals, from K. D. Jadhav's bronze at Helsinki 1952 to Aman Sehrawat's bronze at Paris 2024.
