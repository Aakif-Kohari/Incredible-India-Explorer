Begum Rokeya

Pioneering Writer, Educator & Reformer (1880–1932)

Begum Rokeya Sakhawat Hossain was a pioneering writer, educator, and social reformer whose lifetime contribution transformed women's education in colonial Bengal. Defying social confinement to study secretly by candlelight, she devoted her life to building the Sakhawat Memorial Girls' School and the Islamic Women's Association. Her utopian story *Sultana's Dream* (1905) remains one of the world's earliest works of feminist science fiction, advocating for female agency, scientific progress, and social equality.

📚 Quick Facts

  • Lifespan 9 December 1880 – 9 December 1932
  • Birthplace Pairaband, Rangpur, Bengal Presidency
  • Major Institutions Sakhawat Memorial Girls' School & Anjuman-e-Khawateen-e-Islam
  • Key Writings Sultana's Dream (1905), Padmarag (1924), Oborodh-Basini (1931)
  • Annual Honor 'Rokeya Day' celebrated annually on 9 December
