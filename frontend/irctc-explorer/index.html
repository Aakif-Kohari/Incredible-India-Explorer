1999 • A new passenger-services corporation

Why IRCTC was created

IRCTC was incorporated on 27 September 1999 as an extended arm of Indian Railways, with a mandate spanning catering, hospitality and tourism.

🏛️

Railway-linked origin

IRCTC was established to upgrade, professionalise and manage catering and hospitality services at stations, on trains and at other locations.

🎫

Passenger access

The corporation became a major digital interface for reserved rail travel, helping move reservation services from counters toward the internet and mobile devices.

🧭

Travel beyond tickets

Its remit also includes tourism, budget accommodation, tour packages and related travel services, connecting railway journeys with destination experiences.

From infrastructure to interfaceIRCTC's story is less about replacing the railway and more about making railway services easier to reach.
