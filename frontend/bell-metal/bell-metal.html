Bell Metal Craft

Explore traditional metal casting, artisan techniques, products, and cultural uses.

History

Bell metal, an alloy primarily of copper and tin, has been used in the Indian subcontinent for thousands of years. Its unique resonance makes it the traditional choice for bells, while its purported health benefits make it highly valued for utensils.

Assam: Sarthebari is renowned for its Kah (bell metal) utensils, heavily patronised by the Ahom kings.
Odisha: The Kansari artisans of Odisha have a rich heritage in crafting intricate temple bells and household items using traditional lost-wax casting and forging.
Chhattisgarh: Bastar's Dhokra art heavily features bell metal, creating rustic, primitive, and intricate tribal motifs using the cire-perdue (lost wax) technique.
