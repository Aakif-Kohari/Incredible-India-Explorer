Malpe Beach

A stunning natural natural port and golden sand beach known for its vibrant coastline and seamless connectivity to St. Mary's Islands.

Location & Beach Overview

  • Location: Udupi District, Karnataka, India (approx. 6 km from Udupi city)
  • Overview: Malpe is a major port and a pristine beach destination famous for its endless stretches of golden sand, swaying palm trees, and safe swimming waters.

Coastal Landscape

The coastline features smooth golden sands bounded by rocky outcrops, clear blue waters of the Arabian Sea, and scenic estuary views where the Udayavara River empties into the sea.

Marine & Recreational Activities

Visitors can enjoy an array of water sports including banana boat rides, jet skiing, parasailing, and surfing. The beach is also home to a thriving fishing harbor with bustling local boat activity.

Island Connections & Nearby Attractions

Island Connections

Regular ferry services operate from Malpe to the geological wonder of St. Mary's Islands, famous for its unique hexagonal basaltic rock formations.

Nearby Attractions

Explore the Vadabhandeshwara Balarama Temple, St. Mary's Islands, Malpe Sea Walkway, and the historic Daria-Bahadurgad Fort.
