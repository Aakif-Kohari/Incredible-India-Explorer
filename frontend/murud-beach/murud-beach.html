Murud Beach

A serene coastal haven renowned for its dark sand shoreline, gentle waves, and historic views of the formidable sea fortress of Murud-Janjira.

Location & Beach Overview

  • Location: Raigad District, Maharashtra, India (Konkan coastline)
  • Overview: Murud Beach is a picturesque and tranquil destination stretching along the Konkan belt, offering panoramic vistas of the Arabian Sea and easy access to coastal heritage landmarks.

Coastal Landscape

The coastline is characterized by a sweeping stretch of dark golden-to-brown sand, bordered by dense clusters of coconut and betel nut palms. The shallow waters make it ideal for relaxing walks and scenic coastal views.

Murud-Janjira Heritage Connection

The beach serves as the primary gateway to the legendary Murud-Janjira Fort—an architectural marvel situated on an oval-shaped rock island in the Arabian Sea. Famous for remaining unconquered by various colonial powers, including the British and Portuguese, it adds immense historical depth to the region.

Nearby Attractions & Visitor Activities

Nearby Attractions

Explore the Murud-Janjira Fort, Ahmedganj Palace, Kasa Fort, and nearby pristine beaches like Kashid Beach.

Activities

Enjoy boat rides to Janjira Fort, camel and horse rides on the shoreline, water sports, and tasting authentic Konkani coastal cuisine.
