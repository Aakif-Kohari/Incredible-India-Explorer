Kantha Embroidery

Explore traditional Bengali textile art, layered stitching techniques and patterns.

Historical Overview

Kantha is one of the oldest forms of Indian embroidery, originating in Bengal. It is historically practiced by rural women to recycle old saris and dhotis, layering them and stitching them together to create light blankets, quilts, and garments. It represents thrift, resourcefulness, and a profound form of self-expression for Bengali women, whose intricate stitches often narrated folklore, myths, and village life.
