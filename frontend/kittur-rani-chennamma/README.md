# ⚔️ Kittur Rani Chennamma: The Warrior Queen

## Overview
A comprehensive historical profile of **Kittur Rani Chennamma** (1778-1829), the Queen of Kittur who led one of the first armed rebellions against the British East India Company in 1824, more than three decades before the Great Uprising of 1857.
