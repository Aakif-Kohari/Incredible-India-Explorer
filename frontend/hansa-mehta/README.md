# ⚖️ Hansa Mehta: Architect of Equality

## Overview
A comprehensive historical profile of **Hansa Mehta** (1897-1995), Indian freedom fighter, educator, Constituent Assembly member, and the woman who changed "all men are born free" to "all human beings are born free" in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

- **Human Rights Work:** 6 features and 6 UN contributions including UDHR

## Historic Achievement
Changed UDHR Article 1 from "All men are born free and equal" to "All human beings are born free and equal" - one of the most consequential language edits in human rights history.
