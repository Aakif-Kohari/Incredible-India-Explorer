Krishnaraja Wadiyar IV (Mysore's Progressive Maharaja)

Discover Maharaja Krishnaraja Wadiyar IV — the visionary who transformed the Princely State of Mysore into India's premier model state through pioneering education, Asia's first hydro-electric station, the iconic KRS Dam, and modern industries.

Explore His Legacy ↓

Educational Pioneers & Research Institutes

Believing that education was the cornerstone of progress, the Maharaja made primary education free and compulsory in 1916. He generously funded the establishment of world-class institutions that continue to drive India's scientific and cultural advancement today.

Infrastructure & Industrial Marvels

With the brilliant engineering mind of Diwan Sir M. Visvesvaraya, Mysore became a pioneer in industrialization. From Asia's first hydro-electric project to the establishment of iron and steel works, the Maharaja laid the foundation for modern Karnataka's economy.

Cultural Heritage & Mysore Palace

A great patron of arts, literature, and music, Krishnaraja Wadiyar IV oversaw the reconstruction of the magnificent Amba Vilas Palace after the old wooden palace burned down in 1897. His court was a melting pot of classical Carnatic music, Mysore painting, and scholarly pursuits.

Reign & Governance Timeline

A chronological journey through the defining moments of a reign that balanced tradition with relentless modernization, earning Mysore the title of the "Model State" of British India.

The Enduring Legacy of the Rajarshi

Krishnaraja Wadiyar IV passed away in 1940, but his vision outlived him. He left behind a state with the highest literacy rate in India, a robust industrial base, and a rich cultural identity. Mahatma Gandhi, upon visiting Mysore, famously remarked that the Maharaja was a "Rajarshi" (a saintly king) and that Mysore was a "model state" for the rest of India to emulate.
