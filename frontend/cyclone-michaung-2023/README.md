# Cyclone Michaung 2023 — Storm Dossier & Urban Flooding Case Study

An interactive educational dossier and case study documenting **Severe Cyclonic Storm Michaung (December 2023)**, its extreme rainfall and flooding over Chennai and northern Tamil Nadu, its landfall near Bapatla (Andhra Pradesh), infrastructure breakdowns, and state/national disaster management responses.

## Features
- **Key Meteorology & Impact Statline**: 100 km/h peak winds, 985 hPa central pressure, ~151+ mm/24h rainfall over Chennai, 17–18 casualties, and $1.3B estimated damages.
- **Interactive Track Map & Timeline Scrubber**: 6-waypoint schematic SVG map with scrub slider and next/prev buttons tracing genesis to landfall and dissipation.
- **Regional Impact Breakdown**: Parallel comparative analysis of rainfall-induced urban inundation in Tamil Nadu vs wind/coastal landfall damage in Andhra Pradesh.
- **Observed Rainfall Distribution**: Daily rainfall progression and intensity comparison chart.
- **Urban Flooding Anatomy**: Analysis of flat coastal topography, wetland encroachment, and drainage challenges in the Chennai basin.
- **Interactive Knowledge Quiz**: 4-question interactive quiz testing comprehension of meteorological dynamics and relief operations.
- **Theme Switcher & Accessible Navigation**: Dark/Light mode toggle with `localStorage` memory and full keyboard accessibility.
- **Schema.org Structured Data**: JSON-LD `Article`, `Event`, and `Place` metadata for semantic indexing.

## File Structure
- `index.html`: Semantic HTML5 markup, SVG track map, structured data.
- `style.css`: Responsive CSS with dark/light themes, card layouts, and animations.
- `script.js`: Interactive engine for track scrubber, infocards, and quiz widget.
- `README.md`: Profile documentation and technical overview.
