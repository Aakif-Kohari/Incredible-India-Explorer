Dr. E. K. Janaki Ammal

Pioneer of Cytogenetics & Defender of Silent Valley

Dr. Edavalath Kakkat Janaki Ammal (1897–1984) was one of India's most brilliant plant scientists. From developing high-sucrose sugarcane hybrids in Coimbatore and co-authoring the landmark Chromosome Atlas of Cultivated Plants in London, to reorganizing the Botanical Survey of India and spearheading the preservation of Silent Valley, her research reshaped plant science across the globe.

🌿 Quick Facts

  • Lifespan 4 Nov 1897 – 7 Feb 1984
  • Birthplace Thalassery, Kerala
  • Doctorate D.Sc., University of Michigan (1931)
  • Key Achievements Sugarcane Hybrids, Chromosome Atlas, BSI Director-General
  • National Honor Padma Shri (1977)
