🦖 Locality Profile

Raiyoli, Balasinor (Gujarat)

Late Cretaceous (~68 – 66 Million Years Ago)
India's Jurassic Park

World-renowned as India’s premier dinosaur graveyard, Raiyoli preserves thousands of fossilized bones and clutches of dinosaur eggs in the Lameta Formation. Apex predator Rajasaurus narmadensis was excavated here.

📍 Coordinates: 22.98° N, 73.35° E (Mahisagar, GJ)
🪨 Geological Formation: Lameta Formation (Calcareous Sandstone)
🔬 Key Fossils: Rajasaurus, Rahiolisaurus, Titanosaur clutches
🏛️ On-Site Museum: Balasinor Dinosaur Museum & Fossil Park
