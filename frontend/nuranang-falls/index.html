Nuranang Falls

The 100-meter high-altitude cascade of the Sela Pass region. A Himalayan marvel in Arunachal Pradesh.

Identity & Verification

Often ambiguously referred to as "Sela Waterfall" or "Jang Falls," the verified name of this cascade is Nuranang Falls. It is formed by the Nuranang Chu, a glacial stream that descends dramatically before joining the Tawang River.

Geographic Accuracy

Located in the Tawang district of Arunachal Pradesh, the falls sit at an elevation of approximately 3,000 meters (9,800 ft). It is situated about 40 km from Tawang town and just 2 km from the town of Jang, making "Jang Falls" a common local misnomer.

Elevation & Himalayan Setting

Water Source

The Nuranang Chu originates from the melting snows and glaciers of the eastern Himalayas near the Sela Pass (4,170m). The water is ice-cold year-round and carries fine glacial silt, giving it a distinctive turquoise hue in the summer months.

Seasonal & Temperature Effects

Route Explorer & Accessibility

Reaching Nuranang Falls requires navigating the challenging, high-altitude roads of the Sela Pass. The route is only accessible during specific months due to heavy snowfall.
