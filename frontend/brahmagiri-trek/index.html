Brahmagiri Peak Trek ബ്രഹ്മഗിരി / ବ୍ରହ୍ମଗିରି — Shola Forest & Peak (1,608m)

Nestled on the border of Coorg (Karnataka) and Wayanad (Kerala), Brahmagiri towers 1,608 meters above pristine Shola tropical evergreen forests. Experience sacred waterfalls, subterranean bird caves, wild elephant corridors, and 360-degree Western Ghats vistas.

Permit Rules & How to Reach

📋 Mandatory Forest Department Permits

  • Entry Permits: Must be obtained on-site at Iruppu Forest Range Office.
  • Mandatory Escort: A Forest Department eco-guard accompanies every trekking group.
  • Strict Regulations: No plastic littering, smoking, or loud noise allowed inside wildlife sanctuary.

🚗 How to Reach Base

  • From Madikeri (Coorg): 60 km via Gonikoppal & Kutta to Iruppu Falls.
  • From Mysore: 110 km via Hunsur & Nagarahole National Park route.
  • From Wayanad (Kerala): 35 km from Mananthavady to Thirunelli / Kurchi.
