Discover Tarsar Marsar Trek

Tarsar Marsar is a premier alpine lake trek nestled in the Kolahoi mountain range of Jammu & Kashmir. Starting from the beautiful village of Aru near Pahalgam, the trail climbs through subalpine valleys and coniferous forests along the Lidder River, opening up to spectacular grassy valleys populated by nomadic shepherds.

The defining highlight is the twin glacial lakes: Tarsar Lake (3,790m) and Marsar Lake (3,800m). Tarsar is a striking almond-shaped lake where trekkers are permitted to pitch camps directly on the meadow shores. Just across the mountain pass lies Marsar Lake, frequently blanketed in swirling mists, feeding the Dagwan river flowing towards Dachigam National Park.

Major Attractions & Highlights

💡 Quick Trek Tips

  • Permit Requirements: Wildlife entry permits must be registered at the Aru checkpost. Carry multiple copies of your government ID.
  • Camping Rules: Respect local ecology. Camp only on designated turf areas around Tarsar Lake; avoid dumping litter in or near the water.
  • Nomadic Gujjar Interaction: Shekwas and Lidderwat house seasonal nomadic mud huts. Be respectful of their livestock and dogs.
  • Best Time: July to early September offers lush green meadows, blooming flowers, and clear blue lake water.

Trek Route & Day-wise Highlights

The standard trail climbs from Aru base to Lidderwat forests, exits the tree-line into Shekwas pastures, and climbs the moraine to Tarsar and Marsar lakes.
