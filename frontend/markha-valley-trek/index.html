Trek profile

Markha Valley at a glance

The Markha Valley route is one of Ladakh's best-known multi-day treks, linking mountain settlements with high-altitude passes and broad views toward Kang Yatse and the surrounding ranges.

LocationLadakh, IndiaIndus–Markha region
DifficultyModerate to difficultHigh altitude and long days
Distance~65–80 kmVaries by itinerary
Duration6–9 trekking daysPlus acclimatization and transfers
Best seasonJune–SeptemberRoute conditions permitting
Common startSpituk / ZingchenApproach varies by itinerary

Landscape

Desert mountains, green villages

The trail moves between stark scree slopes, narrow river corridors, willow-lined settlements and open high-altitude pastures. The contrast between irrigated village fields and barren mountain faces is a defining feature of the trek.

High passesKongmaru La and other route variants provide dramatic Himalayan panoramas.
River crossingsThe Markha River and tributaries can require careful timing and local guidance.
Mountain viewsClear weather can reveal Kang Yatse, Stok Kangri region peaks and distant ranges.
Village lifeTraditional settlements provide a window into Ladakhi agriculture and architecture.

Planning note

Altitude is the main challenge

Several sections sit above 4,000 m, with a high point around Kongmaru La depending on the route. Build in acclimatization before the trek and do not ignore altitude symptoms.

Route

Major villages and landmarks

Camp names and exact walking distances differ between operators and route variants. This sequence represents the commonly described Markha Valley experience.

01

Leh acclimatization

Spend sufficient time adjusting to altitude before beginning the trekking itinerary. The approach and first camp depend on the selected route.

02

Spituk / Zingchen approach

Many itineraries approach the trail from the west near Spituk or Zingchen, entering the mountain valleys away from Leh.

03

Yurutse and Rumbak

Small settlements and high grazing landscapes introduce the trek's characteristic mix of cultivated valley floors and dry mountains.

04

Ganda La

The Ganda La crossing is a major early high point on the classic west-to-east route, followed by descent toward the Markha watershed.

05

Markha Valley villages

Markha, Hankar and nearby settlements feature stone homes, irrigated fields, streams and views of the surrounding peaks.

06

Nimaling

Nimaling is a high grazing meadow and a memorable camp area beneath the Kang Yatse massif.

07

Kongmaru La

The high pass provides a demanding climb and, in good visibility, broad views across the Ladakh and Karakoram landscapes.

08

Shang Sumdo / Chokdo

The route descends through a changing landscape before connecting back toward the road network and onward travel.

Mountain passes

Where the route gets serious

Ganda La

A major pass on the classic route, with steep approaches and exposed high-altitude terrain.

Altitude: ~4,980 m

Kongmaru La

Often considered the route's signature pass, with a sustained climb from Nimaling and dramatic views on clear days.

Altitude: ~5,260 m

Route variations

Alternative approaches can change the number of passes, camps and total distance. Follow the itinerary issued by your operator.

Local culture

Travel through living Ladakhi landscapes

Village agriculture

Markha Valley settlements use irrigation to grow barley, peas and other crops in a short summer growing season. Green fields stand out against the surrounding arid terrain.

Gompas and chortens

Mani walls, chortens and Buddhist religious spaces occur along the route. Walk respectfully, follow local signs and avoid touching religious objects.

Traditional homes

Stone-and-earth houses, prayer flags and compact village lanes reflect adaptations to the cold, dry Himalayan environment.

Hospitality

Homestays and tea houses can connect visitors with local families. Ask before photographing people, homes or religious ceremonies.

Safety

Prepare for altitude and remoteness

This is high-altitude trekking. Current weather, local advisories, road conditions and trail information should be checked immediately before departure.

⛰

Acclimatize

Allow adequate time in Leh. Increase sleeping altitude gradually where the itinerary allows and report symptoms early.

💧

Hydrate

Drink regularly and protect water from freezing. Carry purification or treatment appropriate to the route.

🌦

Weather

Mountain weather changes quickly. Carry layers, rain protection and sun protection even during the warmer trekking season.

🥾

Terrain

Expect loose rock, steep descents, stream crossings and long walking days. Use appropriate footwear and poles if useful.

🧭

Stay together

Follow your guide and do not take shortcuts across unfamiliar terrain. Keep the group informed before leaving a camp.

📞

Emergency plan

Connectivity can be limited. Confirm evacuation, communication and emergency arrangements with your operator.
