An Introduction

India, Beyond the Mainland

India's territory extends far beyond its mainland coastline. Two Union Territories — the Andaman & Nicobar Islands in the Bay of Bengal and Lakshadweep in the Arabian Sea — are made up entirely of islands, together with hundreds of smaller river, delta and coastal islands scattered from the Brahmaputra in the northeast to the backwaters of Kerala in the south. Each island group has its own geology, ecology and culture, from volcanic peaks and coral atolls to mangrove deltas and rock-cut cave temples.
