# 🇮🇳 Smart Festival Travel Planner for India

## Overview

The **Smart Festival Travel Planner** is an interactive frontend feature for the Incredible India Explorer project that helps users discover Indian festivals and plan trips around them.

India celebrates hundreds of festivals throughout the year, and each festival offers a unique cultural and travel experience. This feature brings festival dates, locations, cultural information, activities, nearby attractions, and travel planning together in one place.

Users can browse upcoming festivals, filter them by month, state, region, and category, view detailed festival information, generate a basic travel itinerary, and save their favourite festivals for future reference.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Festival Calendar** – Explore festivals throughout the year.
- 🔎 **Festival Search** – Search festivals by name, location, or description.
- 🗓️ **Month Filtering** – Browse festivals based on the month.
- 📍 **State Filtering** – Find festivals celebrated in a particular state.
- 🧭 **Region Filtering** – Explore festivals by Indian region.
- 🏷️ **Category Filtering** – Filter festivals based on their cultural category.
- 🎉 **Upcoming Festivals** – Highlight festivals that are coming up.
- 📖 **Festival Details** – View dates, location, description, significance, and activities.
- 🗺️ **Destination Recommendations** – Discover nearby attractions associated with a festival.
- 🧳 **Travel Plan Generation** – Generate a basic festival-based itinerary.
- ❤️ **Favourite Festivals** – Save festivals for future reference.
- 💾 **LocalStorage Support** – Favourite festivals persist across page refreshes.
- ✅ **Travel Checklist** – Get a useful preparation checklist before travelling.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Works across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
- ⚠️ **Empty States** – Provides helpful feedback when no festivals match the selected filters.

---

## 🎊 Festival Categories

The planner supports multiple festival categories:

- Religious
- Cultural
- Traditional
- Harvest
- Music
- Dance
- Food
- National
- Seasonal

A festival can belong to more than one category.
