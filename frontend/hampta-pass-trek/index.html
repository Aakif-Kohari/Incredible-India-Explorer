Hampta Pass Trek

A dramatic Himalayan crossover from the green Kullu Valley to the stark landscapes of Lahaul, with a high pass, alpine meadows, river crossings and optional Chandratal detours.

From lush meadows to a cold-desert valley

Hampta Pass is a classic crossover route in Himachal Pradesh. The trail starts near Manali on the Kullu side, climbs through forest and alpine meadows, crosses the high Hampta Pass, and descends into the drier Lahaul landscape.

The route is commonly described as moderate. The pass day is the most demanding because of altitude, steep terrain, loose rock and potentially snowy conditions. Weather and river-crossing conditions can change quickly, so current local guidance should always take priority.

At a glance

  • Location: Kullu–Lahaul, Himachal Pradesh
  • Starting point: Jobra, via Manali
  • Duration: 5 days / 4 nights
  • Distance: about 24 km on a standard itinerary
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Best season: June to September
  • High point: about 4,270 m
