ORIGIN

From Ahmedabad to Indian homes

Rasna's story began with Pioma Industries in Ahmedabad. The company entered the soft drink concentrate market in the 1970s, initially using the brand name Jaffe.

The brand was renamed Rasna in 1979. Its do-it-yourself format allowed consumers to prepare a fruit-flavoured drink at home by combining concentrate with water and sugar.

The value-oriented model helped Rasna establish a distinctive position in India's beverage market.
