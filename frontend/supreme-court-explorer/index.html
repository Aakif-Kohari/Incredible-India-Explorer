📜 History & Foundation

1937

Federal Court of India

Established under the Government of India Act 1935 in the Chamber of Princes, Parliament House.

1950

Inauguration of Supreme Court

Inaugurated on Jan 28, 1950 under Article 124, replacing Privy Council appeals with sovereign Indian jurisdiction.

1958

Tilak Marg Building

Moved to present Tilak Marg premises designed in Indo-British classical style with scales of justice layout.

2019

Expansion to 34 Judges

Sanctioned strength expanded to CJI + 33 judges to tackle increasing constitutional & appellate caseloads.

🏛️ Constitutional Jurisdiction & Powers

⚖️
Article 131

Original Jurisdiction

Exclusive power to adjudicate direct legal disputes between Union Government and States or between States.

📜
Article 32

Writ Jurisdiction (Heart & Soul)

Guarantees citizens direct access for Fundamental Rights enforcement via 5 Writs (Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, etc.).

🏛️
Article 136

Special Leave Petition (SLP)

Discretionary appellate power to grant leave to appeal against any judgment from any court/tribunal in India.

💡
Article 143

Advisory Jurisdiction

Power of the President of India to consult the Supreme Court on questions of law or public importance.
