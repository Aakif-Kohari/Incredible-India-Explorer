## Overview

## Key Contributions
- First woman arrested in Salt Satyagraha (1930)
- President, All India Women's Conference
- Chairperson, All India Handicrafts Board (25+ years)
- Founded Sangeet Natak Akademi, National School of Drama
- Established Crafts Council of India
- Revived dying craft traditions across India
