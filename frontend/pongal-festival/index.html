Pongal: Tamil Nadu's Harvest

Celebrate the sun, the soil, and the cattle. Explore the four-day thanksgiving festival that marks the beginning of the Tamil month of Thai.

The Four-Day Celebration

Pongal is uniquely structured over four consecutive days, each dedicated to a specific aspect of nature, gratitude, and social bonding.

Historical Timeline

Kolam & Decorative Traditions

The threshold of every Tamil home is adorned with intricate Kolams (geometric patterns drawn with rice flour) during Pongal. These designs welcome prosperity and feed tiny insects, reflecting a deep ecological consciousness.

Pongal Delicacies

The festival is named after the signature dish "Pongal" (meaning "to boil over"), which is prepared in a new clay pot outdoors and allowed to spill over, symbolizing abundance and prosperity.
