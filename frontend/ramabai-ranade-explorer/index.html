Ramabai Ranade

Pioneer of Seva Sadan & Defender of Women's Rights

Ramabai Ranade (1862–1924) was one of India's most influential social reformers. Co-founding the Arya Mahila Samaj (1882) and establishing the historic Seva Sadan (1908–1909), she pioneered formal nursing, midwifery, and teacher training for women across Maharashtra. Her leadership led petitions for female voting rights in 1919 and transformed women's education in Western India.
