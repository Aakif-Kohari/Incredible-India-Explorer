# Chandrabhaga Beach Explorer — Odisha

## Overview
An interactive destination profile for **Chandrabhaga Beach** in Puri district, Odisha — India's first Blue Flag certified shoreline located just 3 km from the UNESCO World Heritage Konark Sun Temple.

## Key Features & Highlights
- **Sun God Surya Heritage & Wheel Motif:** Signature animated rotating Konark Sun Wheel SVG and historical solar connections.
- **Legends & Cultural Festivals:** Samba's healing penance, Chandrabhaga's sacrifice, Magha Saptami Chandrabhaga Mela, and the International Sand Art Festival.
- **Konark Heritage Corridor Route:** Walking & driving sequence linking Chandrabhaga, Konark Sun Temple, Ramachandi Temple, Kuruma, and Puri Jagannath Temple.
- **Curated Multi-Day Itineraries:** 1-Day Konark Sunrise Trail, 2-Day Golden Triangle (Puri & Konark), and 3-Day Heritage & Chilika Lagoon Circuit.
- **Visual Photo Gallery & Modal Lightbox:** Interactive gallery with image credits, keyboard navigation (`←`/`→`/`Esc`), and enlarged photo views.
- **Full SEO & Schema.org Structured Data:** Semantic HTML5 and JSON-LD `@type: "Beach"` markup.
