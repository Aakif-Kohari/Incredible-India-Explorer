Discover Kedarkantha Trek

Kedarkantha is a spectacular high-altitude summit located in the Govind Wildlife Sanctuary of Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand. Ascending to 3,800 meters, it is widely considered India's most popular winter snow trek. Flanked by deep pine glades and wind-swept ridges, the trail is famous for its outstanding campsites and beginner-friendly slopes.

The journey begins at the wooden hamlet of Sankri and winds upwards through dense forests of oak, pine, and blooming red rhododendrons. Trekkers camp beside the legendary lake of Juda Ka Talab, which freezes completely during peak winter. From the Kedarkantha base ridge camp, a midnight summit scramble leads to the peak marker dedicated to Lord Shiva, yielding a breathtaking 360-degree sunrise view of Swargarohini, Bandarpoonch, Black Peak, and Kalanag ranges.

Major Attractions & Highlights

💡 Quick Trek Tips

  • Permit Requirements: A wildlife sanctuary entry fee and permit must be obtained at the Sankri Forest Department Checkpost.
  • Winter Gear: Spikes and gaiters are mandatory for winter treks to navigate the hard snow packs and ice layers safely.
  • Nearby Excursions: Har Ki Dun Valley, Osla Village heritage, and Mori river activities on the Tons River.
  • Best Time: December to January for deep winter snow, and March to April for fresh alpine springs and blooming rhododendrons.

Trek Route & Day-wise Highlights

The standard trail starts from Sankri base village, climbs to Juda Ka Talab, proceeds to the Base Camp ridge, and culminates at the Kedarkantha summit.
