Meesapulimala Trek മീശപ്പുലിമല — Munnar, Idukki, Kerala

Rising 2,640 meters above sea level near Munnar in the High Ranges of Idukki, Meesapulimala is the second-highest peak in the Western Ghats and South India. Renowned for its sweeping montane grasslands, deep Shola forest valleys, spring Rhododendron blooms, and breathtaking cloud-sea views, Meesapulimala offers one of Kerala's most iconic alpine treks.

South India's 2nd Highest Summit (2,640m)

Meesapulimala derives its name from eight high mountain crests resembling the whiskers of a tiger ('Meesa' = moustache/whiskers, 'Puli' = tiger, 'Mala' = mountain). Standing at 2,640 meters (8,661 ft), it is second only to Anamudi (2,695m) in South India.

Montane Biosphere

Grassland & Shola Ecosystem

The landscape is defined by vast rolling montane grasslands interspersed with dense, humid Shola forest valleys. During spring, wild crimson Rhododendron flowers bloom across Rhodo Valley.
