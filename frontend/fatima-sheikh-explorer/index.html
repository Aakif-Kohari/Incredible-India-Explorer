Fatima Sheikh

Pioneering Educator & Social Reformer (19th Century)

Fatima Sheikh was one of the most important pioneers of women's education in modern India. Associated with the early girls' education movement in Pune, she worked in close collaboration with Savitribai and Jyotirao Phule. When the Phules were evicted from their home for advocating female literacy, Fatima Sheikh and her brother Usman Sheikh offered them refuge and opened a school inside their Ganj Peth residence. Teaching alongside Savitribai Phule, she defied double social orthodoxy to advocate for marginalized girls' literacy.

📚 Quick Facts

  • Lifespan 19th Century (Active c. 1848 – 1856)
  • Birthplace Pune, Maharashtra, British India
  • Pioneering Milestone One of India's first Muslim female teachers (Mrs. Mitchell's Normal School)
  • Welfare Partner Co-teacher at Bhide Wada & Usman Sheikh's Residence Schools
