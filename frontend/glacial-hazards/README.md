# Glacial Hazards of the Himalayas

An interactive educational profile exploring glaciers, glacial lakes, and hazards associated with rapidly changing Himalayan environments.

## Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Himalayan Map
- 8 critical features including glaciers and glacial lakes
- Filter by feature type (critical, retreating, disaster)
- Filter by region (Uttarakhand, Himachal, Sikkim)
- Click markers for detailed information

### 🧊 Glacier Science
- Glacier formation process
- Glacial lake formation stages
- Himalayan glacier geography
- Retreat rates and trends

### ⚠️ Hazard Types
- GLOF (Glacial Lake Outburst Floods)
- Ice avalanches
- Glacier surges
- Ice debris flows

### 📚 Historical Disasters
- 2013 Kedarnath disaster
- 2021 Chamoli disaster
- 2023 South Lhonak GLOF
- Historical context and lessons

### 🛰️ Monitoring Systems
- Satellite monitoring (ISRO, NASA, ESA)
- Ground sensors and early warning
- Research institutions
- Community preparedness

### 🛡️ Risk Reduction
- Engineering solutions
- Policy frameworks
- Community preparedness

## Statistics

- **Total Glaciers**: 9,575 in Indian Himalayas
- **Glacial Lakes**: 12,000+ identified
- **Critical Lakes**: 200+ classified as critical
- **Average Retreat Rate**: 15-20 m/year
