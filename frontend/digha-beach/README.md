# Digha Beach Profile — Brighton of the East

## Overview
An interactive destination profile exploring **Digha Beach** in Purba Medinipur, West Bengal — the Bay of Bengal's famous wide, shallow seaside resort renowned for dual sunrise-and-sunset vistas, dense casuarina groves, low-tide sand flats, and fresh seafood culture.

## Key Features & Enhancements
- **Tide Dynamics & Sunset Guide**: Live/simulated calculation of low-tide flats vs. high-tide sea surges and dual-horizon sun watching.
- **Interactive Multi-Day Itinerary Planner**: Switch between 1-Day Express, 2-Day Classic Weekend, and 3-Day Coastal Circuit with timeline flows.
- **Coastline Comparison Matrix**: Detailed comparison of Old Digha, New Digha, Udaipur, Talsari, and Shankarpur.
- **Interactive Leaflet Map**: Multi-point geographical exploration covering beaches, ZSI Marine Aquarium, Amarabati Park, and transit hubs.
- **Filterable Visual Gallery with Modal Lightbox**: Multi-category filtering and full keyboard-navigable lightbox with previous/next controls.
- **Ambient Ocean Soundscape (Web Audio API)**: Zero-dependency, offline-friendly synthesized ocean wave sound player.
- **Dark/Light Mode Synchronized**: Integrated with the application's global theme controller.
- **One-Click Share & Toast Feedback**: Native Web Share API with clipboard fallback.
- **Semantic SEO & Schema.org Structured Data**: Accessible and compliant HTML5 markup.
