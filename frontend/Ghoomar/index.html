Ghoomar

A graceful Rajasthani dance of circular movement, flowing ghagras and rhythmic claps, closely associated with celebration and community.

A living tradition

Movement that becomes celebration

Ghoomar is traditionally performed by women in Rajasthan. Its circular turns, graceful hand movements and rhythmic steps create a visual rhythm in which the costume becomes part of the dance.

ORIGIN

Rajasthan

Closely associated with Rajasthan's folk culture and community celebrations.

FORM

Circular movement

Dancers move in circles with coordinated steps, turns, claps and gestures.

OCCASIONS

Festivals & weddings

Traditionally seen at weddings, festivals and other celebratory gatherings.

IDENTITY

Costume in motion

The swirling ghagra and odhani create the dance's distinctive visual character.

Performance context

More than a costume and a step

01

History & community

Ghoomar developed within Rajasthan's folk performance culture and became closely connected with community celebrations and women's social gatherings.

02

Dress & adornment

Bright ghagras, cholis, odhanis and jewellery contribute to the visual effect. Patterns, fabrics and ornamentation can vary by community and occasion.

03

Music & rhythm

Folk songs and percussion support the dancers' repeated steps and turns. The musical setting can vary with the performers and occasion.

04

Regional variation

Rajasthan contains many local communities and performance traditions, so choreography, dress, songs and presentation are not identical everywhere.
