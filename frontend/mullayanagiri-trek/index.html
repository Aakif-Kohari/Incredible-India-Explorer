Route & elevation profile

Sarpadhari — meaning "serpent path" for its zig-zag bends — is the trailhead. From here the climb is short but honest: steep at the start, easing as you near the top.

0.0 KM · START

Sarpadhari trailhead

A white arch marks the start. The initial climb is the steepest, on inclines of 60° or more in places.

~1.2 KM

Nandi statue

A rock-sheltered Nandi (bull) idol beneath a tree, roughly the halfway landmark on the climb.

Summit Information

The roof of Karnataka

At 1,925 m (6,316 ft), Mullayanagiri is the highest point in Karnataka and the 23rd highest peak in the Western Ghats. It forms part of the Chandra Drona Hill Ranges within the Baba Budangiri range, and looks out over the Chikkamagaluru coffee country below.

The summit takes its name from a small shrine — a gadduge, or tomb — dedicated to the sage Mullappa Swamy, who is said to have meditated in the caves just below the peak. Local Kannada tradition also links the name to mullai, an old word for forest.

A short walk from the temple, a police radio relay station occupies the highest ground itself. On a clear day the summit gives an unbroken view across the Western Ghats, with the neighbouring peak of Seethalayyanagiri visible close by.

Scenic viewpoints

Moments along the climb worth slowing down for.

🌄

Summit panorama

360° views of the Western Ghats, rolling Shola grasslands, and the ridgelines of the Baba Budangiri range stretching to the horizon.

🌫️

Mist line at Sarpadhari

Fast-moving cloud and sunshine trade places within minutes along the switchbacks — bring a layer for the sudden chill.

🌅

Sunrise from the ridge

An early start from Sarpadhari rewards trekkers with the sun breaking over the valley before the day-trip crowds arrive.

☕

Coffee country below

Looking down from the mid-climb switchbacks, the estates and Shola forest patches of Chikkamagaluru unfurl in a green-on-green quilt.

🐂

Nandi rock shelter

A quiet, shaded stop roughly halfway up, framed by rugged boulders — a natural pause point with valley views back the way you came.

🌌

Clear-night skies

On dry-season nights, minimal light pollution near the peak makes for notable stargazing — though trekking after dark isn't advised.

Nearby attractions

Worth combining with the trek if you have a full day, or a weekend, in Chikkamagaluru.

Baba Budangiri

A Sufi shrine dedicated to Baba Budan (Guru Dattatreya), reachable by a ridge trek from Mullayanagiri over grassland and steep descents.

~10–12 km trek

Manikyadhara Falls

A modest waterfall along the Baba Budangiri trail, historically the only reliable water source between the two peaks.

~9 km

Kemmangundi

A cooler hill-station retreat further along the ridge system, popular for its gardens and viewpoints over the Bhadra valley.

Half-day by road

Bhadra Wildlife Sanctuary

A Project Tiger reserve in the Chikkamagaluru district, home to leopards, tigers, and over 250 recorded bird species.

Day trip from Chikmagalur

Coffee Gallery, Chikmagalur

Run by the Coffee Board of India, this museum traces the region's coffee-growing history from bean to cup.

~20 km, Dasarahalli

Chikkamagaluru town

The gateway town for the trek, with homestays, local eateries, and onward transport to Bangalore and Birur Junction.

~20 km from summit

Find the trailhead

Sarpadhari sits on the Chikmagalur–Baba Budangiri road, about 15 km from town.

Trailhead

Sarpadhari, Chikkamagaluru

13.390972° N, 75.721390° E

From Chikmagalur town, take the road toward Baba Budangiri. At Kaimara (~8 km), turn left; a Y-junction shortly after leads directly to the Mullayanagiri road and Sarpadhari.

Pack for the climb

  • Sturdy trekking shoes
  • Water & light snacks
  • Light jacket or windbreaker
  • Cap, sunscreen, first-aid basics

Good to know

  • No entry fee for Mullayanagiri
  • No permit required for this peak
  • No water source on the main trail
  • Avoid trekking after dark

Getting there

  • Chikmagalur town: ~20 km away
  • Bengaluru: ~250–280 km by road
  • Nearest rail: Birur Junction
  • Buses & jeeps run to Sarpadhari

Onward trails

  • Baba Budangiri (~10–12 km)
  • Manikyadhara Falls (~9 km)
  • Kemmangundi (via ridge)
  • Deviramma Temple (~13 km)
