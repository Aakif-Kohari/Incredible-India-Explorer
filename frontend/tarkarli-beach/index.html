Tarkarli: Konkan's Crystal Coast

Dive into Maharashtra's clearest waters. Explore pristine beaches, coral reefs, and the vibrant Malvani culture of the Konkan coast.

Maharashtra's Scuba Diving Capital

Located in the Sindhudurg district of Maharashtra, Tarkarli Beach is renowned for its crystal-clear waters with visibility extending up to 20 feet. The beach sits at the confluence of the Karli River and the Arabian Sea, creating a unique estuarine ecosystem.

Geographic Location

Coordinates: 16.02°N, 73.49°E
District: Sindhudurg, Maharashtra
Coastline: Konkan Coast, Arabian Sea
Nearest City: Malvan (8 km), Mumbai (540 km)
Best Season: October to May (clear waters, calm seas)

Tarkarli has emerged as India's premier scuba diving destination outside of the Andaman Islands, with PADI-certified dive centers offering courses and guided dives to explore coral reefs, shipwrecks, and diverse marine life.

The Konkan Coast Context

The Konkan coast stretches 720 km from Mumbai to Goa, characterized by laterite plateaus, coconut groves, mango orchards, and pristine beaches. Tarkarli represents the best of Konkan's natural beauty combined with rich cultural heritage.

Malvani Coastal Culture

The Malvani culture of the Konkan coast is deeply connected to the sea. The local cuisine, traditions, and way of life revolve around fishing, coconut cultivation, and the rhythms of the Arabian Sea.
