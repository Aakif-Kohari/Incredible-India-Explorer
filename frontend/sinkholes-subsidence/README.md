# Sinkholes and Ground-Subsidence Hazards in India

An interactive educational profile exploring sinkholes and ground subsidence hazards across India, with detailed case studies and mitigation strategies.

## Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Hazard Map
- 8 sinkhole and subsidence zones across India
- Filter by type (sinkhole, subsidence, mining)
- Filter by severity level
- Clickable markers with detailed zone information

### 🕳️ Formation Processes
- 4-stage formation visualization
- Sinkhole types (cover-collapse, cover-subsidence, dissolution, piping)
- Land subsidence mechanisms

### ⚠️ Cause Categories
- **Geological**: Karst topography, cave systems, fault zones, volcanic activity
- **Hydrological**: Over-extraction, aquifer depletion, water chemistry, seasonal fluctuations
- **Mining**: Coal mining, abandoned mines, sand mining, limestone quarrying
- **Urban**: Construction, infrastructure, water infrastructure, unplanned development

### 📍 Vulnerable Regions
- **Himalayan Foothills**: Joshimath subsidence (700+ buildings affected)
- **Coal Mining Regions**: Jharia coal fires (40 km² affected)
- **Coastal Plains**: Chennai groundwater crisis (15m water table drop)
- **Arid Regions**: Rann of Kutch salt karst

### 📚 Case Studies
- 2023 Joshimath Subsidence (Uttarakhand)
- 2020+ Jharia Coal Field Fires (Jharkhand)
- 2019 Chennai Groundwater Crisis (Tamil Nadu)
- 2018 Raniganj Coal Field (West Bengal)
- 2017 Rann of Kutch (Gujarat)
- 2016 Delhi Metro Tunnel (Delhi)

### 🔍 Monitoring Systems
- Satellite monitoring (InSAR, ISRO RISAT, Sentinel-1)
- Ground-based sensors (GPS, tiltmeters, extensometers)
- Geophysical surveys (GPR, electrical resistivity)
- Data analysis systems (machine learning, early warning)

### 🛡️ Mitigation Strategies
- Engineering solutions (grouting, pile foundations)
- Groundwater management (extraction limits, artificial recharge)
- Policy & regulation (land-use zoning, building codes)
- Community safety (risk mapping, early warning)
