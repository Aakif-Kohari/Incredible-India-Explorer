# Desertification and Land Degradation in India

An interactive educational profile exploring desertification and land degradation affecting 96.4 million hectares of India's land.

## Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Degradation Map
- 8 most affected states with severity classification
- Filter by severity (severe >30%, moderate 20-30%, low <20%)
- Filter by region (Western, Central, Southern India)
- Click markers for detailed state information

### 🏜️ Desertification Science
- Definition and types of degradation
- 4-stage degradation cycle
- Soil erosion, salinization, waterlogging

### ⚠️ Causes Analysis
- **Human Activities**: Deforestation, overgrazing, unsustainable agriculture
- **Climate Factors**: Erratic rainfall, droughts, temperature rise

### 📊 Impact Assessment
- Agricultural losses (₹82,000 Cr annually)
- Water scarcity affecting 600 districts
- Dust storms and air quality
- Social impacts and migration

### 🌱 Restoration Strategies
- Afforestation & Green India Mission
- Water conservation (Jal Shakti Abhiyan)
- Sustainable agriculture (Zero Budget Natural Farming)
- Government initiatives (UNCCD commitment)

### ✅ Success Stories
- Sadhguru's Rally for Rivers
- Hiware Bazar model (Maharashtra)
- Tarun Bharat Sangh (Rajasthan)

## Statistics

- **Total Degraded Area**: 96.4 million hectares
- **Percentage Degraded**: 29.3% of India's land
- **Annual Economic Losses**: ₹82,000 Crore
- **Dryland Area**: 68% of India
- **Restoration Target**: 26M hectares by 2030
