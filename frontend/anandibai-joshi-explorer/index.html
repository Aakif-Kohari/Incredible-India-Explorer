Dr. Anandibai Joshi

First Indian Woman Physician (M.D., Philadelphia 1886)

Dr. Anandibai Gopalrao Joshi (1865–1887) was one of India's earliest female doctors. Inspired by the tragic loss of her newborn infant at age fourteen due to lack of medical care, she braved ocean voyages and social excommunication to graduate with an M.D. from the Woman's Medical College of Pennsylvania in 1886. Her thesis on Indian obstetrics pioneered scientific maternal healthcare in India.
