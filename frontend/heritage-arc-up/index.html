🚆 Quick Travel & Circuit Info

Essential guidance for planning your journey along the UP Heritage Arc.

🚗

Recommended Route

Agra → Lucknow → Varanasi

Total Circuit Distance: approx. 700 km via Yamuna & Agra-Lucknow Expressways and NH19.

⏱️

Ideal Duration

6 to 8 Days

Spend 2 days in Agra, 2-3 days in Lucknow, and 3 days experiencing Varanasi and Sarnath.

🚅

Connectivity

Vande Bharat & Express Railways

High-speed rail links and expressways connect all three hubs with frequent daily service.

🌤️

Best Season

October to March

Pleasant winter weather ideal for sightseeing, heritage walks, and evening Ganga Aarti.
