# Gargi Vachaknavi — The Philosopher Who Challenged the Scholars of Ancient India

## Overview
An interactive historical and philosophical profile dedicated to **Gargi Vachaknavi (गार्गी वाचक्नवी)** — the renowned Vedic philosopher, scholar, and *brahmavādinī* from the **Brihadaranyaka Upanishad** (*Yajnavalkya-Kanda*, Chapter 3), who famously engaged sage Yajnavalkya in profound metaphysical debate at the royal court of King Janaka of Videha.

## Key Features & Highlights
- **Evidence-Based Historiographical Filter:** Interactive toggle separating verbatim primary Upanishadic text evidence from later commentarial traditions and modern retellings.
- **Two Rounds of Philosophical Dialectics:** Step-by-step documentation of the Janaka court assembly, the initial cosmological regress through cosmic spheres, and the second challenge on the ultimate warp and weft (*otaśca protaśca*) of existence.
- **Interactive Cosmological Dialectic Hierarchy:** Clickable 8-tier hierarchy detailing the cosmic layers from Water (Āpaḥ) to the Imperishable Ground (*Akṣara Brahman*).
- **Primary Sanskrit Verses & Translations:** Includes the seminal declaration of the *Akṣara* from Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 3.8.8.
- **Interactive Comprehension Check Widget:** Instant feedback quiz testing grasp of the textile metaphor and Vedic epistemological methods (*neti neti*).
- **Women in Ancient Indian Philosophy Survey:** Comparative contextual analysis covering Maitreyī, the term *Brahmavādinī*, and Rigvedic women seers (Lopāmudrā, Ghoṣā).
- **Full SEO & Schema.org JSON-LD Structured Data:** Semantic HTML5 with `@type: "Article"` and `@type: "Person"` metadata.
