Space and ISRO

A journey through India’s extraordinary space achievements, scientific milestones, and exploration of the Moon, Mars, and beyond.

ISRO Overview

From launching sounding rockets from a humble church in Thumba to exploring the lunar south pole and studying the Sun, the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has transformed global space exploration. Its contributions span communication satellites, weather forecasting, navigation, planetary science, and the upcoming human spaceflight program.
