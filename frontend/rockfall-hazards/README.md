# Rockfall Hazards in India's Mountain Regions

An interactive educational profile exploring rockfall hazards across India's mountain regions including the Himalayas and Western Ghats.

## Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Hazard Map
- 8 high-risk rockfall zones across mountain regions
- Filter by region (Himalayas, Western Ghats)
- Filter by risk level (high, moderate)
- Clickable markers with detailed zone information

### 🪨 Rockfall Science
- 4-stage formation process visualization
- Geological factors explanation
- Trigger categories (rainfall, seismic, thermal, human)
- Regional examples with case studies

### ⚠️ Notable Incidents
- 2021 Chamoli Rock-Ice Avalanche
- 2023 Joshimath Landslides
- 2017 Kedarnath Highway Rockfall
- 2014 Malin Landslide
- 2013 Kedarnath Disaster
- 2010 Leh Cloudburst

### 🛡️ Mitigation Strategies
- Engineering solutions (rock bolts, netting, galleries)
- Monitoring systems (InSAR, GPS, LiDAR)
- Prevention measures (slope stabilization, drainage)
- Community safety programs

### 🏛️ Government Initiatives
- National Landslide Risk Mitigation Centre
- Landslide Hazard Zonation (0.42M km² mapped)
- National Remote Sensing Centre
- Early Warning Systems
