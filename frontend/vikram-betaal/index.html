Vikramaditya and Betaal

Core Theme: Righteousness (Dharma), Judgment, Wisdom Key Figures: King Vikramaditya & Betaal

Welcome to the interactive explorer of the Baital Pachisi (Twenty-Five Tales of Betaal), one of ancient India’s most enduring literary treasures.

The Cosmic Vow: To fulfill a promise to a mysterious ascetic, King Vikramaditya must capture Betaal, a celestial vampire-spirit hanging from a banyan tree, and carry him in complete silence across the wilderness.

The Psychological Game

1

The Condition

"If you speak a single word during our journey, I will fly back to my tree!"

2

The Riddle

Betaal narrates a complex tale to pass the time.

3

The Trap

At the climax, Betaal poses a profound moral riddle, challenging the King's judgment.

4

The Catch

If the King knows the answer but stays silent, his head shatters. If he speaks the truth, Betaal escapes.

Did You Know?

The Throne of Vikramaditya

The legendary Singhasan Battisi possessed 32 living statuettes that would only allow a ruler of equal virtue and justice to sit upon it.

The Navaratnas

His royal court pioneered the "Nine Jewels" advisory system, including the legendary classical Sanskrit poet Kalidasa.

The Vikram Samvat Calendar

The ancient Indian historical lunisolar calendar, widely used today, is named in honor of his historical ascension in 57 BCE.
