मालवी • 𑂧𑂰𑂪𑂫𑂲 (Mālvī / Malwa Heritage)

Explore Malvi—a melodious Western Indo-Aryan language spoken by over 16 million people across the fertile black-soil Malwa plateau of Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan. Celebrated for its unique 'छे / छा' copula, Maach folk theatre on open stages, Sanjha wall art, Kabir bhajans on the Tambura, and culinary hospitality of Daal-Bafla.

Devanagari Script (देवनागरी लिपि)

Devanagari is the primary literary writing system of Malvi. Used extensively in modern collections of Maach plays, Kabir bhajan anthologies, regional periodicals, and radio dramas.

मालवा धरती गहन गंभीर, पग-पग रोटी डग-डग नीर।
📖

Historical Mahajani & Kaithi (महाजनी)

For centuries, merchant guilds and royal accountants across Ujjain, Mandu, and Ratlam utilized Mahajani shorthand for rapid bookkeeping and commercial correspondence across the Malwa trade route.

राम राम भाया, सब कुशल मंगल छे।
