Cornelia Sorabji

First Female Advocate of India & Champion of Purdahnashin Rights

Cornelia Sorabji (1866–1954) was a groundbreaking legal pioneer. She became the first woman to read law at Oxford University (BCL 1892), served two decades as Legal Advisor to the Court of Wards protecting over 600 secluded purdahnashin women and orphans, and was admitted in 1923 as India's first female High Court Advocate.
