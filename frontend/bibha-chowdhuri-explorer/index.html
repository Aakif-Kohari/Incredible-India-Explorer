Dr. Bibha Chowdhuri

Pioneering Indian Woman Physicist (1913–1991)

Dr. Bibha Chowdhuri was an Indian physicist whose research at the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS) and the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR) helped establish India's cosmic-ray and particle-physics program. Working with photographic nuclear emulsions at high altitudes, she co-authored landmark Nature papers in the 1940s — predating Cecil Powell's Nobel Prize-winning pion discovery — and became the first woman scientist at TIFR under Homi J. Bhabha.
