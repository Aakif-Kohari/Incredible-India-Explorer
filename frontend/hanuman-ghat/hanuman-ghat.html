Varanasi Ghat Corridor

Hanuman Ghat

A tranquil riverfront dedicated to devotion, classical music legends, and timeless spiritual heritage along the Ganges.

Southern Ghats, Varanasi, UP River Ganges

History & Heritage

Roots anchored in ancient devotion and legendary saints

Hanuman Ghat, historically known as Rameswaram Ghat, stands as a cornerstone of the southern ghat corridor in Varanasi. Its lineage is deeply intertwined with legendary figures of Indian bhakti movement and classical heritage. According to local lore and sacred texts, the ghat was originally consecrated by Lord Rama himself, establishing a link to the Rameswaram pilgrimage.

Over centuries, the ghat transformed into a vibrant cultural hub, famously associated with the great saint-poet Tulsidas, who is believed to have lived and wrestled near these sacred grounds before composing his immortal epics.

Quick Facts

  • Ancient Name: Rameswaram Ghat
  • Corridor: Southern Varanasi Riverfront
  • Key Association: Saint Tulsidas & Sri Vaishnava traditions
  • Cultural Milestone: Hub of classical Hindustani vocalists (Ustad Bismillah Khan associations)

Hanuman Traditions & Worship

The abode of strength, loyalty, and unwavering faith

Shrine of Lord Hanuman

At the center of the ghat lies the revered shrine dedicated to Lord Hanuman, revered as the protector and guardian deity of the southern riverfront stretch.

Akhada Culture

Hanuman Ghat historically bridges spiritual devotion with physical discipline, housing traditional wrestling (akhada) traditions dedicated to Bajrangbali.

Daily Rituals

Pilgrims gather at dawn and dusk for synchronized ringing of brass bells, chanting of the Hanuman Chalisa, and traditional riverfront aartis.

Sacred Temples

Architectural marvels anchoring spiritual life

Mukhimata & Hanuman Shrine

Housing ancient idols carved from stone, these shrines attract seekers looking for strength, courage, and spiritual refuge.

Sri Rameswaram Temple

Linked directly to the historical roots of the ghat, facilitating symbolic southern pilgrimage rites right on the banks of Ganga.

Festivals & Devotional Practices

When the ghat comes alive with light and music

Hanuman Jayanti and Nag Panchami witness exuberant celebrations where thousands congregate. Additionally, the ghat is famous for morning musical gatherings (Prabhāt Feri) and classical mehfils honoring its rich artistic heritage.

Architectural Character

Stone steps, carved facades, and multi-tiered structures

Unlike the crowded commercial ghats further north, Hanuman Ghat features wide, weathered stone staircases (pakka ghat) flanked by multi-storied traditional residential houses, ashrams, and ornate shrines built in classic North Indian vernacular style. The masonry harmonizes seamlessly with the natural sweep of the crescent Ganges.
