Kumara Parvatha Trek Highest Peak in Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary (1,712m)

A demanding and incredibly rewarding expedition through the Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary in the Western Ghats. Known for its endless grasslands, volcanic rock formations, and steep ascents, this trek tests endurance while offering unmatched panoramic views of Karnataka's lush landscapes.

Discover Kumara Parvatha Trek

Located in the Dakshina Kannada and Kodagu districts, Kumara Parvatha is the second-highest peak in Kodagu and the highest in the Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary. The trek is widely considered one of the toughest in South India, requiring peak physical fitness.

The trail features a dramatic transition from dense, humid Shola forests to vast, windswept grasslands. Trekkers pass key landmarks like Bhattara Mane, Kallu Mantapa, and the false peak Sheshaparvatha before making the final strenuous push to the summit.

Major Attractions & Highlights

💡 Quick Trek Tips

  • Permit Requirements: Mandatory forest department permits required, usually obtainable near Bhattara Mane.
  • Leech Menace: Post-monsoon, the lower forest trails are heavily infested with leeches. Carry salt and use proper gaiters.
  • Camping: Camping is prohibited at the summit. Trekkers generally camp near Bhattara Mane or the forest office.
  • Best Time: October to February offers clear skies and pleasant weather, avoiding the harsh monsoons and summer heat.

Trek Route & Stage Highlights

The standard trail starts from Kukke Subramanya Temple, climbs to Bhattara Mane, crosses Kallu Mantapa and Sheshaparvatha, and finishes at the main peak.

🚨 Essential Safety Guidelines

Despite being in South India, the Kumara Parvatha trek is notorious for its steep gradients, heat, and physical exhaustion risks. Adhere to these guidelines to ensure a safe trek:

1. Heat Exhaustion and Dehydration

The stretch from Bhattara Mane to Sheshaparvatha is entirely exposed to the sun. Combined with the steep incline, dehydration and heatstroke are severe risks. Carry at least 3-4 liters of water and electrolyte salts.

2. Slippery Terrain & Leeches

The forest section before Bhattara Mane consists of mud and roots that become dangerously slippery if it rains. Proper grip trekking shoes are non-negotiable. Additionally, prepare for leech encounters in this moist zone.

3. Sheshaparvatha Ledges

The final push from Sheshaparvatha to the main summit involves navigating narrow ledges alongside steep drops. Maintain three points of contact and do not attempt this section in heavy rain or low visibility.

⚠️ Strict Forest Rules

The Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary enforces strict rules. Plastics are strictly banned and heavily checked at the forest checkpoint. Camping is ONLY allowed near Bhattara Mane. Do not trespass into restricted sanctuary zones.
