Shivrajpur Beach

A pristine Blue Flag certified beach renowned for its crystal-clear turquoise waters, white sandy shores, and vibrant marine ecosystem.

Location & Beach Overview

  • Location: Near Dwarka, Devbhumi Dwarka District, Gujarat, India
  • Overview: Shivrajpur Beach is an internationally recognized Blue Flag beach, celebrated for its immaculate cleanliness, pristine white sands, safety standards, and eco-friendly tourism initiatives.

Natural and Marine Features

The beach features gentle wave action, crystal-clear shallow waters, and a rich marine ecosystem home to coral reefs, colorful fish species, and occasional sightings of dolphins and sea turtles along the coast.

Activities & Nearby Attractions

Activities

Enjoy scuba diving, snorkeling, glass-bottom boat rides, swimming, peaceful beach walks, and sunset viewing along the shore.

Nearby Attractions

Explore the holy city of Dwarka, Dwarkadhish Temple, Bet Dwarka, and the historic Shivrajpur Lighthouse.

Visitor Information

  • Best Time to Visit: October to May (pleasant weather and calm sea conditions)
  • Facilities: Well-maintained changing rooms, eco-washrooms, seating umbrellas, lifeguard services, and parking areas.
