# 🦖 Raiyoli Dinosaur Fossils (Balasinor, Gujarat)

Raiyoli in the Mahisagar district of Gujarat represents one of the three largest dinosaur fossil localities on Earth. Preserved within the Late Cretaceous Lameta Formation (~68–66 Million Years Ago), the site holds dense skeletal bone beds and intact sauropod egg clutches.

---

## ⏳ Locality Details & Geological Age
* **Location:** Raiyoli Village, Balasinor Taluka, Mahisagar District, Gujarat, India.
* **Geological Horizon:** Lameta Formation (Calcareous sandstone, marl, chert, and nodular limestone).
* **Chronostratigraphic Age:** Late Cretaceous (Maastrichtian Stage, ~68 to 66 Million Years Ago).

---

## 📍 Primary Dinosaur Discoveries

### 1. *Rajasaurus narmadensis* (Apex Theropod Predator)
* **Classification:** Abelisauridae, Ceratosauria.
* **Dimensions:** 8.5 to 9.0 meters in length; estimated weight 3.0 to 4.0 metric tons.
* **Diagnostic Anatomical Feature:** A distinct single rounded horn crest formed by the fusion of nasal and frontal skull bones.
* **Significance:** Affirms Gondwanan faunal connections with Madagascar (*Majungasaurus*) and South America (*Carnotaurus*).

### 2. *Rahiolisaurus gujaratensis* (Gracile Abelisaurid)
* **Classification:** Abelisauridae.
* **Discovery:** Excavated from a multi-individual bone bed containing seven specimens of varying ages, suggesting gregarious social behavior.

### 3. *Jainosaurus septentrionalis* & *Titanosaurus indicus*
* **Classification:** Titanosauria, Sauropoda.
* **Dimensions:** 15 to 20 meters in length; bulky four-legged herbivorous quadrupeds with osteoderm armor.

---

## 🔬 Historical & Museum Milestones
* **1981–1984 Discovery:** GSI geologist Suresh Srivastava identified bone-rich sedimentary layers during regional mineral mapping.
* **Princess Aaliya Sultana Babi:** Championed conservation and scientific tourism, recognized globally as the "Dinosaur Princess" of Balasinor.
* **2003 Formal Description:** Paul Sereno, Jeffrey Wilson, and Indian colleagues formally described and named *Rajasaurus narmadensis*.
* **Balasinor Dinosaur Museum:** World-class 25,000 sq ft museum facility featuring 10 themed galleries and in-situ fossil bedrock trails.
