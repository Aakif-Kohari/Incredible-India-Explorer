# 🪨 The Lameta Formation and India's Dinosaur Record

The Lameta Formation is India’s most important fossil-bearing rock sequence of the Mesozoic Era, preserving the end-Cretaceous biodiversity of the Indian subcontinent before the Deccan Traps volcanism.

---

## ⏳ Chronostratigraphy & Sedimentary Characteristics
* **Geological Age:** Late Cretaceous (Maastrichtian Stage, ~70 to 66 Million Years Ago).
* **Stratigraphic Position:** Infratrappean to the Deccan Traps (sitting directly beneath basalt lava flows).
* **Lithology:**
  1. **Mottled Nodular Limestone:** Pedogenic calcretes and palaeosols containing dinosaur nesting clutches.
  2. **Green Sandstone & Siltstone:** Fluvial channel deposits yielding articulated sauropod and theropod bones.
  3. **Cherty Limestone:** Shallow freshwater lake beds with gastropods (*Physa prinsepii*) and algae.

---

## 📍 Geographic Distribution
* **Madhya Pradesh:** Jabalpur (Lameta Ghat, Bara Simla, Chui Hill), Dhar, Bagh, Mandu.
* **Gujarat:** Balasinor (Raiyoli), Kheda, and Panchmahal districts.
* **Maharashtra:** Pisdura, Dongargaon, and Umrer.

---

## 🦖 Dinosaur & Vertebrate Taxa
* **Theropoda (Abelisauridae & Noasauridae):**
  * *Rajasaurus narmadensis* (Cranial-horned apex predator).
  * *Indosuchus raptorius* & *Indosaurus matleyi*.
  * *Laevisuchus indicus*.
* **Sauropoda (Titanosauria):**
  * *Isisaurus colberti* (Short-necked, long-armed titanosaur).
  * *Jainosaurus septentrionalis* & *Titanosaurus indicus*.
* **Squamata (Madtsoiidae):**
  * *Sanajeh indicus* (3.5 m nest predator snake).

---

## 🔬 Macro-Evolutionary Importance
* Documents Gondwanan faunal isolation as India drifted across the Tethys Ocean.
* Provides vital data on the environmental degradation caused by Deccan Traps volcanism leading up to the K-Pg extinction event.
