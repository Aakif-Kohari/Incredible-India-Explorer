Indira Gandhi Paryavaran Puraskar

One of India's most prestigious environmental awards recognizing outstanding contributions to environmental protection and sustainable development.

MoEFCC Awarded By
1987 Instituted

History & Legacy

Instituted by the Government of India through the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) in 1987. It was named in honor of former Prime Minister Indira Gandhi, who strongly advocated for environmental conservation during her tenure. The award was established to recognize exceptional contributions toward environmental protection and sustainable development, and to encourage innovation and environmental stewardship across the nation.

Purpose of the Award

The award specifically recognizes measurable, impactful contributions in vital ecological areas, such as:

  • Environmental conservation
  • Biodiversity protection
  • Sustainable development practices
  • Pollution prevention and control
  • Ecological restoration of degraded areas
  • Climate change mitigation and environmental awareness

Eligibility Criteria

  • Individuals: Citizens of India who have made significant environmental contributions.
  • Institutions & Organizations: Companies or collectives operating in India.
  • NGOs: Non-Governmental Organizations focused on ecological work.
  • Research Bodies: Academic and scientific institutions.
  • Community Groups: Local grassroots collectives protecting the environment.

Award Categories

Environmental Conservation

Protecting natural landscapes, water bodies, and forests from degradation.

Sustainable Development

Innovating practices that balance human needs with ecological preservation.

Biodiversity Protection

Safeguarding endangered flora and fauna and their native habitats.

Pollution Control

Developing robust mechanisms to reduce air, water, and soil pollution.

Environmental Research

Advancing scientific understanding of ecological challenges and solutions.

Community Initiatives

Mobilizing local populations for grassroots environmental action.

Environmental Impact Focus

Forest Conservation: Increasing green cover and protecting vital carbon sinks.

Wildlife Protection: Halting poaching and preserving critical wildlife corridors.

Pollution Reduction: Tangible reductions in emissions or hazardous waste.

Resource Management: Efficient use of water, energy, and soil resources.

Climate Resilience: Helping communities adapt to shifting climate patterns.

Education: Spreading deep ecological awareness among the masses.

Interesting Facts

  • 🌿 Named after former Prime Minister Indira Gandhi, honoring her legacy of environmental advocacy.
  • 🏆 Promotes excellence in environmental conservation at both the grassroots and institutional levels.
  • 🤝 Recognizes both individual and institutional achievements equally.
  • 🇮🇳 Encourages sustainable development across all states and territories of India.
  • 💡 Highlights innovative, scalable environmental practices that can be adopted nationwide.
