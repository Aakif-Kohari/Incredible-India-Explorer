The Crucible of India’s Future Cricket Legends.

The Namesake

Col. Cottari Kanakaiya Nayudu

The namesake of this prestigious tournament was none other than India's first Test cricket captain. C.K. Nayudu led India in its inaugural Test match against England at Lord's in 1932. Known for his aggressive batting and immense physical fitness, he was a trailblazer who commanded immense respect worldwide.

The BCCI instituted the Col. C.K. Nayudu Trophy to honor his unparalleled contribution to the sport. Today, it stands as the ultimate stepping stone for youth players aiming to replicate his legendary international career.

A giant among men, his legacy now fuels the dreams of twenty-somethings across the nation.

U-23 Four-Day Format

Forging First-Class Temperament

The tournament operates as the definitive bridge between Under-19 boys' cricket (Cooch Behar Trophy) and the senior professional men's circuit (Ranji Trophy).

The Teams

Comprises all state and regional associations recognized by the BCCI. Divided into Elite and Plate groups based on historical performance, ensuring highly competitive, balanced matchups.

Four-Day Format

Unlike rapid T20 tournaments, the C.K. Nayudu Trophy plays out over grueling four-day matches, aggressively testing the patience, stamina, and red-ball technique of young athletes.

National Selectors

The matches are closely monitored by BCCI talent scouts. High-performing players are frequently fast-tracked to India A squads, IPL franchises, and directly to the Ranji Trophy.
